A slice of pizza has a curved crust and two straight edges meeting at the centre. Its crust and its area are both a fraction of the whole circle, and the angle at the centre tells us which fraction.

## Arc length

:::definition Arc and sector
An **arc** is part of the circumference of a circle. A **sector** is the slice between two radii and an arc. If the angle at the centre is $\theta$, the sector is $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ of the whole circle.
:::

So the arc is $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ of the circumference:
$$\text{arc length} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$$
where $r$ is the radius and $\theta$ is the angle at the centre in degrees.

:::example An arc
Find the length of the arc of a sector with radius $6$ cm and angle $120^\circ$.
@step Arc $= \dfrac{120}{360} \times 2 \times \pi \times 6 = 4\pi = 12.566\ldots$
@answer The arc is about $12.6$ cm long.
:::

:::mistake The perimeter of a sector
The perimeter of a sector is the arc **plus the two radii**. For the sector above it is $12.57 + 6 + 6 = 24.6$ cm, not $12.6$ cm.

## Sector area

The area of a sector is the same fraction of the area of the circle:
$$\text{sector area} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$$

:::example A sector in terms of π
Find the area of the sector above, with radius $6$ cm and angle $120^\circ$, in terms of $\pi$.
@step Area $= \dfrac{120}{360} \times \pi \times 6^2 = \dfrac{1}{3} \times 36\pi$
@answer The area is $12\pi$ cm².
:::

:::definition Segment
A **chord** is a straight line joining two points on a circle. The **segment** is the region between the chord and the arc. Its area is the sector area take away the area of the triangle made by the two radii and the chord, which is $\tfrac{1}{2}r^2 \sin \theta$.
:::

:::

:::summary
- A sector with angle $\theta$ is $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ of the circle.
- Arc length $= \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$, and the perimeter of a sector adds two radii.
- Sector area $= \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$.
- Segment area $=$ sector area $- \tfrac{1}{2}r^2 \sin \theta$.
:::
