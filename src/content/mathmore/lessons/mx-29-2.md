Which water pump is closest to each house in a village? A Voronoi diagram answers that for every point at once, by splitting the map into regions around the pumps.

## Voronoi diagrams

:::definition Voronoi diagram
A **Voronoi diagram** divides a plane with a set of points, called **sites**, into **cells**, one for each site. Each cell contains every point that is closer to its site than to any other site. The cells meet along **edges**, and the edges meet at **vertices**.
:::

To decide which cell a point is in, we find its distance to each site and pick the nearest.

:::example Which cell?
Sites are at $A(1, 1)$, $B(7, 2)$ and $C(3, 8)$. Which cell is the point $(4, 3)$ in?
@step $\sqrt{9 + 4} \approx 3.61$ to $A$, $\sqrt{9 + 1} \approx 3.16$ to $B$, $\sqrt{1 + 25} \approx 5.10$ to $C$
@answer The cell of $B$
:::

## Edges are perpendicular bisectors

A point on the edge between two cells is the same distance from both sites. The points that are equidistant from two sites lie on the **perpendicular bisector** of the segment joining them, so each edge is part of a perpendicular bisector.

:::example The equation of an edge
Find the equation of the edge between $A(2, 1)$ and $B(6, 5)$.
@step The midpoint of $AB$ is $(4, 3)$.
@step The gradient of $AB$ is $\dfrac{4}{4} = 1$, so the edge has gradient $-1$.
@step $3 = -1 \times 4 + c$, so $c = 7$.
@answer $y = -x + 7$
:::

## Vertices and the largest empty circle

A vertex where three cells meet is equidistant from all three of their sites, so it lies on all three perpendicular bisectors. We find it by solving two of the bisector equations together. It is the centre of the circle through the three sites.

:::check mx-voronoi-bisector 3
:::

This gives the solution to a common problem: where should something unwanted, such as a noisy factory, be put so that it is as far as possible from the nearest site? Inside a region bounded by the diagram, the best place is one of the vertices, and its distance to its three sites is the radius of the largest circle with no site inside it. (Points on the boundary of the region also need checking in a full answer.)

:::example The best vertex
A vertex $V(4, 3)$ is equidistant from the sites $A(1, -1)$, $B(8, 6)$ and $C(1, 7)$. How far is it from them?
@step $VA = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}$
@answer $5$ units from each of $A$, $B$ and $C$
:::

:::summary
- Each cell of a Voronoi diagram holds the points nearest to its site.
- Edges are parts of perpendicular bisectors; a vertex is equidistant from three sites.
- The point furthest from its nearest site, inside the region, is at a vertex of the diagram.
:::
