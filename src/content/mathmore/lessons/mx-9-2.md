Speed compares a distance with a time. Density and pressure work the same way: each one is a **compound measure**, made by dividing one quantity by another, and each has a unit made from the two.

## Density

:::definition Density
The **density** of a material is its mass per unit of volume:
$$\text{density} = \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$$
With mass in grams and volume in cm³, density is in g/cm³. A material with a high density, like lead, is heavy for its size.
:::

:::example Finding a density
A piece of stone has a mass of $540$ g and a volume of $200$ cm³. Find its density.
@step Density $= \dfrac{540}{200} = 2.7$
@answer The density is $2.7$ g/cm³.
:::

The formula rearranges to give the other two quantities: mass $=$ density $\times$ volume, and volume $= \dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{density}}$.

:::example Finding a mass
A block of metal is a cuboid $5$ cm by $4$ cm by $2$ cm. The density of the metal is $7.8$ g/cm³. Find its mass.
@step Volume $= 5 \times 4 \times 2 = 40$ cm³.
@step Mass $= 7.8 \times 40 = 312$
@answer The mass is $312$ g.
:::

:::

:::mistake Averaging two densities
When two metals are melted together, the density of the mix is **not** the average of the two densities. Find the total mass and the total volume, then divide: density $= \dfrac{\text{total mass}}{\text{total volume}}$.
:::

## Pressure

:::definition Pressure
**Pressure** is the force pushing on each unit of area:
$$\text{pressure} = \frac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}$$
Force is measured in **newtons** (N). With the area in m², pressure is in N/m², which are also called pascals.
:::

:::example Finding a pressure
A crate exerts a force of $1\,200$ N on the floor over an area of $0.8$ m². Find the pressure on the floor.
@step Pressure $= \dfrac{1\,200}{0.8} = 1\,500$
@answer The pressure is $1\,500$ N/m².
:::

The same force spread over a bigger area gives a smaller pressure, which is why snowshoes stop us sinking into snow.

:::tip Converting cm² to m²
A square $1$ m by $1$ m is $100$ cm by $100$ cm, so $1$ m² $= 10\,000$ cm². To change cm² to m², divide by $10\,000$, not by $100$.
:::

:::check mx-pressure 3
:::

:::summary
- Density $= \dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$, in g/cm³ or kg/m³.
- Pressure $= \dfrac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}$, in N/m².
- Rearrange each formula to find the other two quantities, and check the units match before dividing.
- $1$ m² $= 10\,000$ cm².
:::
