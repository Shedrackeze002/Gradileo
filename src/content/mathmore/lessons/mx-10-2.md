A goat tied to a post can reach every point within the length of its rope. The set of all those points is a shape, and describing that shape is what loci are about.

## Describing a locus

:::definition Locus
A **locus** (plural **loci**) is the set of all the points that obey a rule. The rule can describe a line or curve, such as "exactly $3$ cm from $P$", or a **region**, such as "less than $3$ cm from $P$".
:::

There are four loci we use again and again:

| Rule | Locus | How to construct it |
|---|---|---|
| A fixed distance from a point | a circle | compasses, centred on the point |
| The same distance from two points (**equidistant**) | the perpendicular bisector | compasses, arcs from each point |
| The same distance from two lines | the bisector of the angle between them | compasses, arcs from each line |
| A fixed distance from a line segment | two parallel lines joined by semicircles | ruler and compasses |

## Areas of regions

:::example The goat
A goat is tied to a post by a rope $5$ m long. Find the area of grass it can reach.
@step The region is a circle with radius $5$ m.
@step Area $= \pi \times 5^2 = 78.539\ldots$
@answer The goat can reach about $78.5$ m².
:::

:::check mx-locus-region 1
:::

When the goat is tied to the corner of a building, the walls block part of the circle, so we work out what fraction of the circle is left.

:::check mx-locus-region 2
:::

:::example Within a distance of a wall
A sensor covers the ground less than $3$ m from a wall $10$ m long, on both sides. Find the area it covers.
@step The region is a rectangle $10$ m by $6$ m, with a semicircle of radius $3$ m at each end.
@step Rectangle: $10 \times 6 = 60$ m². The two semicircles make a circle: $\pi \times 3^2 = 28.27\ldots$ m².
@answer The area is about $60 + 28.27 = 88.3$ m².
:::

:::check mx-locus-region 3
:::

:::summary
- A locus is the set of points that obey a rule.
- A fixed distance from a point gives a circle; equidistant from two points gives the perpendicular bisector; equidistant from two lines gives the angle bisector.
- A fixed distance from a line segment gives two parallel lines joined by semicircles.
- "Less than" gives a region inside the locus; find its area by splitting it into rectangles and parts of circles.
:::
