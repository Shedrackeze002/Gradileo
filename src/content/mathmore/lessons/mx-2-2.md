Scientific notation is made for calculating with very large and very small numbers. The exponent laws do most of the work: the coefficients are worked out as ordinary numbers, and the powers of ten are combined by adding or subtracting exponents.

## Multiplying and dividing

:::definition Multiplying and dividing in scientific notation
$$(a \times 10^{m})(b \times 10^{n}) = (a \times b) \times 10^{m+n}$$
$$\frac{a \times 10^{m}}{b \times 10^{n}} = \frac{a}{b} \times 10^{m-n}$$
If the new coefficient is not from $1$ to $10$, adjust it and the exponent.
:::

:::example Multiplying
Find $(3 \times 10^{4})(2.5 \times 10^{6})$ in scientific notation.
@step Multiply the coefficients: $3 \times 2.5 = 7.5$.
@step Add the exponents: $10^{4} \times 10^{6} = 10^{10}$.
@answer $7.5 \times 10^{10}$
:::

:::example Adjusting the coefficient
Find $(6 \times 10^{-3})(4 \times 10^{8})$ in scientific notation.
@step $6 \times 4 = 24$ and $10^{-3} \times 10^{8} = 10^{5}$, so the product is $24 \times 10^{5}$.
@step $24$ is not less than $10$: $24 = 2.4 \times 10^{1}$.
@step So $24 \times 10^{5} = 2.4 \times 10^{1} \times 10^{5} = 2.4 \times 10^{6}$.
@answer $2.4 \times 10^{6}$
:::

:::check mx-sci-multiply 1
:::

:::example Dividing
Find $\dfrac{1.2 \times 10^{5}}{4 \times 10^{-2}}$ in scientific notation.
@step Divide the coefficients: $1.2 \div 4 = 0.3$.
@step Subtract the exponents: $10^{5} \div 10^{-2} = 10^{5 - (-2)} = 10^{7}$.
@step $0.3$ is less than $1$: $0.3 = 3 \times 10^{-1}$, so $0.3 \times 10^{7} = 3 \times 10^{6}$.
@answer $3 \times 10^{6}$
:::

:::mistake Multiplying the exponents
$10^{4} \times 10^{6}$ is $10^{10}$, not $10^{24}$. When powers of the same base are multiplied, their exponents are **added**.
:::

## Adding and subtracting

Coefficients can be added or subtracted only when the powers of ten are the same, just as $3$ tens and $4$ tens make $7$ tens. If the powers differ, rewrite one number first.

:::example Adding with different powers
Find $4.2 \times 10^{5} + 3.1 \times 10^{4}$ in scientific notation.
@step Rewrite the second number with $10^{5}$: $3.1 \times 10^{4} = 0.31 \times 10^{5}$.
@step Add the coefficients: $4.2 + 0.31 = 4.51$.
@answer $4.51 \times 10^{5}$
:::

:::example Subtracting
Find $2.3 \times 10^{-3} - 8 \times 10^{-4}$ in scientific notation.
@step Rewrite the second number with $10^{-3}$: $8 \times 10^{-4} = 0.8 \times 10^{-3}$.
@step $2.3 - 0.8 = 1.5$.
@answer $1.5 \times 10^{-3}$
:::

:::mistake Adding the exponents
$2 \times 10^{3} + 3 \times 10^{3}$ is $5 \times 10^{3}$ (that is, $2000 + 3000 = 5000$), not $5 \times 10^{6}$. Exponents are added only when powers are **multiplied**.
:::

:::summary
- To multiply, multiply the coefficients and add the exponents; to divide, divide the coefficients and subtract the exponents.
- To add or subtract, first write both numbers with the same power of ten, then add or subtract the coefficients.
- Finish by adjusting the coefficient to be from $1$ to $10$, changing the exponent to match.
:::
