Slice a loaf of bread straight across and every slice has the same shape. Slice an orange and every slice is a circle, small near the ends and largest through the middle. The flat face left by a slice is a cross-section, and its shape depends on both the solid and the direction of the cut.

## Cross-sections

:::definition Cross-section
A **cross-section** is the two-dimensional shape where a plane cuts through a solid.
:::

:::definition Cross-sections of prisms and cylinders
A cut **parallel to the base** of a prism or a cylinder gives a copy of the base: a rectangle for a rectangular prism, a triangle for a triangular prism, a circle for a cylinder. A cut **perpendicular to the base** gives a rectangle.
:::

:::example Slicing a cylinder
A cylinder is cut (a) parallel to its base, and (b) perpendicular to its base through its axis. Name each cross-section.
@step (a) A slice parallel to the base is a copy of the base: a circle.
@step (b) A vertical slice through the axis runs the full height and the full diameter: a rectangle.
@answer (a) A circle. (b) A rectangle, the diameter by the height.
:::

## Pyramids, cones and spheres

A cut parallel to the base of a pyramid or a cone gives a smaller copy of the base, shrinking towards the apex. A cut perpendicular to the base through the apex gives a triangle. Every cut through a sphere gives a circle, and the largest circle comes from a cut through the centre.

:::mistake A slanted cut is not the same
A cut at a slant can give other shapes: slicing a corner off a cube gives a triangle, and a slanted cut across a cylinder gives an oval (an ellipse). Read the direction of the cut carefully.
:::

## The area of a cross-section

Once the shape and its dimensions are known, its area follows from the usual formulas.

:::example The area of a cross-section
A cylinder has radius $5$ cm and height $12$ cm. Find the area of the cross-section (a) parallel to the base and (b) through the axis, perpendicular to the base.
@step (a) A circle of radius $5$ cm: $\pi \times 5^2 = 25\pi \approx 78.5\ \text{cm}^2$.
@step (b) A rectangle $10$ cm (the diameter) by $12$ cm: $10 \times 12 = 120\ \text{cm}^2$.
@answer (a) About $78.5\ \text{cm}^2$. (b) $120\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::

:::

:::summary
- A cross-section is the flat shape where a plane cuts a solid.
- A cut parallel to the base of a prism or cylinder is a copy of the base; parallel to the base of a pyramid or cone, it is a smaller copy.
- A cut perpendicular to the base gives a rectangle for prisms and cylinders, and a triangle (through the apex) for pyramids and cones.
- Every cross-section of a sphere is a circle.
:::
