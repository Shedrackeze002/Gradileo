Table salt is not made of sodium atoms and chlorine atoms. It is made of charged particles called ions. Atoms become ions by losing or gaining electrons, and they do it to reach the same stable arrangement as a noble gas.

## How ions form

:::definition Ion
An **ion** is an atom (or group of atoms) that has **lost or gained electrons**, so it has an electric charge.
- **Metal** atoms **lose** their outer electrons and become **positive ions**.
- **Non-metal** atoms **gain** electrons to fill their outer shell and become **negative ions**.
:::

Atoms lose or gain electrons until they have a **full outer shell**, like the nearest noble gas.

:::example A sodium ion
Sodium has the arrangement 2, 8, 1. What ion does it form?
@step Losing its 1 outer electron leaves 2, 8, a full outer shell like neon.
@step It now has 11 protons but only 10 electrons: one more + than −.
@answer $\text{Na}^{+}$, with the arrangement 2, 8.
:::

:::example A chloride ion
Chlorine has the arrangement 2, 8, 7. What ion does it form?
@step Gaining 1 electron gives 2, 8, 8, a full outer shell like argon.
@step It has 17 protons and 18 electrons: one more − than +.
@answer $\text{Cl}^{-}$, called a **chloride** ion.
:::

The charge on an ion follows from the group:

| Group | Outer electrons | Ion charge | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1+ | $\text{Li}^{+}$, $\text{Na}^{+}$, $\text{K}^{+}$ |
| 2 | 2 | 2+ | $\text{Mg}^{2+}$, $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ |
| 13 | 3 | 3+ | $\text{Al}^{3+}$ |
| 15 | 5 | 3− | $\text{N}^{3-}$ |
| 16 | 6 | 2− | $\text{O}^{2-}$, $\text{S}^{2-}$ |
| 17 | 7 | 1− | $\text{F}^{-}$, $\text{Cl}^{-}$ |

Negative ions of single elements take the ending **-ide**: oxide, sulfide, chloride.

## Counting particles in an ion

:::definition Protons never change
Forming an ion changes only the **electrons**. The number of protons, and so the element, stays the same.

$$\text{electrons} = \text{protons} - \text{charge}$$
:::

:::example The oxide ion
How many protons and electrons are in $\text{O}^{2-}$? (Oxygen has atomic number 8.)
@step Protons = 8.
@step The charge is −2, so electrons = 8 − (−2) = 10.
@answer 8 protons and 10 electrons.
:::

:::mistake A positive ion has lost electrons, not gained protons
$\text{Mg}^{2+}$ is positive because it has two **fewer electrons** than protons. Its protons are unchanged.
:::

:::summary
- An **ion** is a charged atom: metals lose electrons (positive ions), non-metals gain them (negative ions).
- Atoms form ions to get a full outer shell, like a noble gas.
- Group 1: 1+, group 2: 2+, group 13: 3+, group 15: 3−, group 16: 2−, group 17: 1−.
- Protons never change; electrons = protons − charge.
:::
