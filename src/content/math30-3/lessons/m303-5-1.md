A rental shop charges a flat fee plus so much per hour. A tractor burns the same amount of diesel every hour. A taxi meter adds the same amount for every kilometre. When something changes by the same amount for each step, it is a **linear relation**, and its graph is a straight line.

## Linear patterns in tables

:::definition Linear relation
A relation is **linear** when $y$ changes by the same amount every time $x$ changes by the same amount. Its graph is a straight line.
:::

Amara rents a skid steer from a rental shop. The table shows the cost.

| Time (h) | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost (\$) | 60 | 100 | 140 | 180 | 220 |

:::example Is the rental cost linear?
Decide whether the relation in the table is linear.
@step The time goes up by $1$ hour each column.
@step The cost changes by $100 - 60 = 40$, $140 - 100 = 40$, $180 - 140 = 40$ and $220 - 180 = 40$.
@step Every change is the same.
@answer The relation is linear: the cost goes up by \$40 for every hour.
:::

:::mistake A linear relation does not have to start at 0
The rental costs \$60 even for $0$ hours, so the line does not pass through $(0, 0)$. It is still linear: what matters is that the **changes** are equal.
:::

## Rate of change and initial value

:::definition Rate of change and initial value
The **rate of change** is how much $y$ changes for each $1$ unit of $x$:
$$\text{rate of change} = \dfrac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}$$
It always has units, such as dollars per hour or litres per kilometre. A decreasing relation has a negative rate of change.

The **initial value** is the value of $y$ when $x = 0$: a flat fee, a starting amount, a full tank.
:::

For the skid steer, the rate of change is \$40 per hour and the initial value is \$60, the flat fee.

:::example Diesel in a tractor
A tractor on a grain farm starts with a full tank. Find the rate of change and the initial value.

| Time (h) | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diesel left (L) | 400 | 364 | 328 | 292 | 256 |
@step Each column, the time goes up by $2$ hours and the diesel changes by $364 - 400 = -36$ litres.
@step Rate of change: $\dfrac{-36}{2} = -18$ litres per hour.
@step The initial value is the diesel at $0$ hours: $400\ \text{L}$.
@answer The rate of change is $-18\ \text{L}$ per hour (the tractor burns $18\ \text{L}$ an hour) and the initial value is $400\ \text{L}$.
:::

:::mistake Forgetting to divide by the step
When the $x$-values go up by $2$, a change of $-36$ is for **two** hours. Divide by the change in $x$ to get the rate for one hour.
:::

## Reading a graph

On a graph, the rate of change is $\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}$ between two points, and the initial value is where the line crosses the vertical axis.

:::example A phone bill
The graph shows Kiona's monthly phone bill for different amounts of data. Find the rate of change and the initial value.
@step Choose two points that sit on grid lines: $(2, 50)$ and $(8, 110)$.
@step Rate of change: $\dfrac{110 - 50}{8 - 2} = \dfrac{60}{6} = 10$ dollars per GB.
@step The line crosses the vertical axis at $30$.
@answer The bill goes up \$10 for each GB, and the initial value is \$30, the monthly fee.
:::

:::tip Pick points far apart
Points far apart on the line give the most accurate rate. Use points where the line crosses grid lines exactly, such as the marked dots.
:::

## When x = 0 is not given

Sometimes you only know two facts. Find the rate first, then work backward to $x = 0$.

:::example A taxi fare
A taxi from the airport costs \$25.00 for a $10\ \text{km}$ trip and \$66.00 for a $30\ \text{km}$ trip. The fare is linear. Find the rate of change and the initial value.
@step Rate of change: $\dfrac{66.00 - 25.00}{30 - 10} = \dfrac{41.00}{20} = 2.05$ dollars per kilometre.
@step The first $10\ \text{km}$ cost $2.05 \times 10 = 20.50$.
@step Initial value: $25.00 - 20.50 = 4.50$
@answer The fare is \$2.05 per kilometre, and the initial value is \$4.50, the flag fee charged before the taxi moves.
:::

:::summary
- A relation is linear when $y$ changes by the same amount for each equal step in $x$. It does not have to start at $0$.
- Rate of change $= \dfrac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}$, with units. It is negative when the relation decreases.
- The initial value is $y$ when $x = 0$. On a graph it is where the line crosses the vertical axis.
- With two facts, find the rate first, then work backward to $x = 0$.
:::
