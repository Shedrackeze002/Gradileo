The sticker on the windshield is only the start. By the time you add GST, fees, interest on the loan, insurance, fuel and repairs, a truck can cost far more than its price. Knowing the real cost before you sign helps you buy a vehicle you can actually afford.

## The price with GST and fees

In many places, a sales tax (called sales tax, GST or VAT) is added to the price of a vehicle and to most dealer fees. Tax rates differ from place to place and can change, so every question in this course states the rate to use. The questions here call the tax GST and use **5%**.

$$\text{total price} = (\text{price} + \text{fees}) \times 1.05$$

Multiplying by 1.05 adds the 5% in one step: $1.05 = 1 + 0.05$.

:::example A used truck
Owen is buying a used half-ton pickup truck for \$24,800, plus a \$499 documentation fee. GST of 5% is charged on the price and the fee. What is the total price?
@step Price and fee: $24{,}800 + 499 = 25{,}299$
@step With GST: $25{,}299 \times 1.05 = 26{,}563.95$
@answer The total price is \$26,563.95.
:::

## Financing: down payment, loan payments and the finance charge

Most people pay part of the price up front and borrow the rest.

:::definition Down payment and finance charge
The **down payment** is the money you pay at the start. The **amount financed** is the part you borrow:
$$\text{amount financed} = \text{total price} - \text{down payment}$$
The **finance charge** is the interest you pay on the loan:
$$\text{finance charge} = \text{total of the payments} - \text{amount financed}$$
:::

:::example Owen’s loan
Owen pays \$3,000 down and finances the rest of the \$26,563.95 at 6.99% a year. His payments are \$564.16 a month for 48 months. Find the amount financed, the total of the payments, the finance charge and the total Owen pays for the truck.
@step Amount financed: $26{,}563.95 - 3{,}000 = 23{,}563.95$
@step Total of the payments: $564.16 \times 48 = 27{,}079.68$
@step Finance charge: $27{,}079.68 - 23{,}563.95 = 3{,}515.73$
@step Total paid: $3{,}000 + 27{,}079.68 = 30{,}079.68$
@answer Owen finances \$23,563.95, the payments total \$27,079.68, the finance charge is \$3,515.73, and the truck costs him \$30,079.68 in all.
:::

:::tip A bigger down payment saves interest
The more you pay down, the less you borrow, and the smaller the finance charge. A shorter loan also costs less interest, but the monthly payments are higher.
:::

## Depreciation

:::definition Depreciation
**Depreciation** is the loss in a vehicle’s value over time. In this course, a vehicle loses a stated percent of its value **each year**, so each year’s value is a percent of the year before:
$$\text{value after } t \text{ years} = \text{price} \times (1 - r)^t$$
where $r$ is the yearly rate as a decimal.
:::

:::example A new SUV
Tanvir buys a new compact SUV for \$38,500 before tax. Suppose it loses 15% of its value each year. What is it worth after 3 years?
@step Losing 15% means keeping 85%, so multiply by 0.85 each year.
@step After 1 year: $38{,}500 \times 0.85 = 32{,}725$
@step After 2 years: $32{,}725 \times 0.85 = 27{,}816.25$
@step After 3 years: $27{,}816.25 \times 0.85 = 23{,}643.8125 \approx 23{,}643.81$, or in one step $38{,}500 \times 0.85^3$
@answer After 3 years the SUV is worth about \$23,643.81. It has lost \$14,856.19 in value.
:::

:::mistake Taking 15% of the original price every year
$15\%$ of \$38,500 is \$5,775, but the SUV does not lose \$5,775 every year. In the second year it loses 15% of \$32,725, which is less. Subtracting $3 \times 5{,}775$ gives \$21,175, which is too low.
:::

## Operating costs: fuel, insurance and maintenance

The costs do not stop once the vehicle is paid for. **Operating costs** include fuel, insurance, maintenance and repairs, and registration.

Fuel use is given in litres per 100 kilometres (L/100 km). A truck that uses 11.6 L/100 km burns 11.6 L for every 100 km it travels.
$$\text{litres} = \dfrac{\text{distance in km}}{100} \times \text{L/100 km} \qquad \text{fuel cost} = \text{litres} \times \text{price per litre}$$

:::example Fuel for a month
Owen drives his truck about 1,800 km a month. It uses 11.6 L/100 km, and fuel costs \$1.49 per litre. What does fuel cost him each month?
@step Hundreds of kilometres: $1{,}800 \div 100 = 18$
@step Litres: $18 \times 11.6 = 208.8$ L
@step Cost: $208.8 \times 1.49 = 311.112 \approx 311.11$
@answer Fuel costs Owen about \$311.11 a month.
:::

:::summary
- Total price = (price + fees) × 1.05 when GST is 5%.
- Amount financed = total price − down payment. Finance charge = total of the payments − amount financed.
- Total cost of buying with a loan = down payment + total of the payments.
- Depreciation at $r$ per year: value = price × $(1 - r)^t$. Do not take the same dollar amount off each year.
- Fuel: litres = (km ÷ 100) × L/100 km; cost = litres × price per litre. Add insurance, maintenance and registration for the full operating cost.
:::
