An equation turns a pricing rule or a rate into a tool: plug in the hours and get the cost, or plug in a budget and find how many hours you can afford. Every linear relation can be written in the same simple form.

## The equation y = mx + b

:::definition Slope-intercept form in context
A linear relation can be written as
$$y = mx + b$$
where $m$ is the **rate of change** (it multiplies the variable) and $b$ is the **initial value** (the value when $x = 0$).

In a real situation, use letters that make sense: $C$ for cost, $h$ for hours, $d$ for distance, $V$ for volume.
:::

:::example A plumber's bill
A plumber charges a call-out fee of \$85 plus \$95 per hour. Write an equation for the cost $C$, in dollars, for $h$ hours of work.
@step The cost goes up \$95 for each hour, so $m = 95$.
@step The call-out fee is charged even for $0$ hours, so $b = 85$.
@answer $C = 95h + 85$
:::

:::mistake Switching the rate and the initial value
$C = 85h + 95$ is wrong. Ask: which number is charged **for each** hour? That number multiplies $h$.
:::

When the amount goes **down**, the rate is negative. A $480\ \text{L}$ tank that loses $12\ \text{L}$ an hour is $V = 480 - 12h$.

:::

## Using an equation

To find $y$, substitute the value of $x$. To find $x$, substitute the value of $y$ and solve.

:::example How much? How many?
Kiona's phone plan is $C = 7.50g + 25$, where $g$ is the gigabytes of data. A delivery driver in the city earns $P = 1.50n + 90$ dollars a day for $n$ packages.

(a) Find Kiona's bill for $6$ GB. (b) How many packages must the driver deliver to earn \$240?
@step (a) $C = 7.50(6) + 25 = 45.00 + 25 = 70.00$
@step (b) $240 = 1.50n + 90$
@step Subtract $90$: $150 = 1.50n$. Divide by $1.50$: $n = 100$.
@answer (a) The bill is \$70.00. (b) The driver must deliver $100$ packages.
:::

:::tip Undo in reverse order
The equation says "multiply by $1.50$, then add $90$". To solve, undo those steps in reverse: subtract $90$, then divide by $1.50$.
:::

:::check m303-linear-equation 1
:::

## Equations from tables and graphs

Find the rate of change from two points and the initial value at $x = 0$, then write $y = mx + b$.

:::example Draining a stock tank
A stock tank on a cattle ranch is drained for cleaning. Write an equation for the water left, $V$ litres, after $t$ minutes.

| Time (min) | 0 | 5 | 10 | 15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water left (L) | 900 | 750 | 600 | 450 |
@step Rate of change: $\dfrac{750 - 900}{5 - 0} = \dfrac{-150}{5} = -30$ litres per minute.
@step Initial value: $900$ litres at $t = 0$.
@answer $V = 900 - 30t$ (the same as $V = -30t + 900$).
:::

:::mistake Losing the negative sign
The water goes **down**, so the rate is negative. $V = 30t + 900$ would say the tank is filling up.
:::

:::check m303-linear-equation 2
:::

## Equations from two facts

:::example Celsius and Fahrenheit
A baker in a mountain town has an oven from the United States. Water freezes at $0^\circ\text{C}$ ($32^\circ\text{F}$) and boils at $100^\circ\text{C}$ ($212^\circ\text{F}$). Write an equation for $F$ in terms of $C$, then convert $25^\circ\text{C}$.
@step Rate of change: $\dfrac{212 - 32}{100 - 0} = \dfrac{180}{100} = 1.8$
@step Initial value: $F = 32$ when $C = 0$.
@step $F = 1.8C + 32$, so $F = 1.8(25) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77$.
@answer $F = 1.8C + 32$, and $25^\circ\text{C}$ is $77^\circ\text{F}$.
:::

Equations also help you compare two offers.

:::example Which rental is cheaper?
Shop A rents a carpet cleaner for \$20 plus \$12 per hour. Shop B charges \$35 plus \$9 per hour. When do they cost the same? Which is cheaper for $8$ hours?
@step Shop A: $C = 12h + 20$. Shop B: $C = 9h + 35$.
@step Same cost: $12h + 20 = 9h + 35$, so $3h = 15$ and $h = 5$.
@step For $8$ hours: Shop A costs $12(8) + 20 = 116$; Shop B costs $9(8) + 35 = 107$.
@answer They cost the same for $5$ hours. For $8$ hours, Shop B is cheaper (\$107 compared with \$116).
:::

:::

:::check m303-linear-equation 3
:::

:::summary
- Write a linear relation as $y = mx + b$: $m$ is the rate of change, $b$ is the initial value.
- Use letters that fit the situation, such as $C = 95h + 85$. A decreasing amount has a negative rate: $V = 900 - 30t$.
- Substitute $x$ to find $y$. Substitute $y$ and undo the operations in reverse to find $x$.
- From a table, graph or two facts: find the rate first, then the initial value.
- To compare two offers, set their equations equal to find where they cost the same.
:::
