On a January night in Grande Prairie, the temperature can fall to $-30^\circ\text{C}$. When you owe someone money, your balance is below zero too. **Integers** help you work with both, and the **order of operations** tells you which step comes first.

## Adding and subtracting integers: a quick review

In Math 10 Essentials you used integers for temperatures and bank balances. Here is a quick reminder before we go further.

:::definition Integer
An **integer** is a whole number that is positive, negative or zero, such as $-8$, $0$ and $15$. Negative numbers are less than zero.
:::

On a number line, adding a positive number moves you right. Adding a negative number moves you left.

:::figure
{"type":"numberLine","min":-8,"max":8,"step":1,"points":[{"value":-6,"label":"start"},{"value":3,"label":"end"}]}
:::

:::example A cold morning in Red Deer
At 7 a.m. it is $-6^\circ\text{C}$. By noon it is $9$ degrees warmer. What is the temperature at noon?
@step Warmer means add: $-6 + 9$
@step Start at $-6$ and move $9$ steps to the right: $-6 + 9 = 3$
@answer At noon it is $3^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::mistake Subtracting a negative
$5 - (-3)$ is **not** $2$. Taking away a negative is the same as adding: $5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8$.
:::

## Multiplying and dividing integers

Now one step further. To multiply or divide two integers, work out the number first. Then decide the sign.

:::figure
{"type":"table","headers":["Signs","Answer","Example"],"rows":[["+ and +","positive","4 × 3 = 12"],["− and −","positive","(−4) × (−3) = 12"],["+ and −","negative","4 × (−3) = −12"],["− and +","negative","(−12) ÷ 3 = −4"]]}
:::

:::tip A short rule
**Same signs: positive. Different signs: negative.** The rule is the same for multiplying and dividing.
:::

:::example A cold front
The temperature in Lethbridge drops $3$ degrees every hour for $5$ hours. What is the total change?
@step A drop of $3$ degrees is a change of $-3$.
@step Multiply without the signs: $5 \times 3 = 15$
@step The signs are different, so the answer is negative: $5 \times (-3) = -15$
@answer The total change is $-15$ degrees.
:::

:::example Dividing two negatives
Calculate $(-36) \div (-4)$.
@step Divide without the signs: $36 \div 4 = 9$
@step The signs are the same, so the answer is positive.
@answer $(-36) \div (-4) = 9$
:::

:::

## The order of operations

:::definition Order of operations
The **order of operations** tells you which step to do first:
1. **B**rackets
2. **E**xponents (powers)
3. **D**ivide and **M**ultiply, from left to right
4. **A**dd and **S**ubtract, from left to right

Many people remember it as **BEDMAS**.
:::

:::example Multiply before you add
Calculate $-7 + 4 \times (-2)$.
@step Multiply first: $4 \times (-2) = -8$
@step Then add: $-7 + (-8) = -15$
@answer $-15$
:::

:::example Brackets first
Calculate $(-10 + 4) \div (-3)$.
@step Brackets first: $-10 + 4 = -6$
@step Then divide: $(-6) \div (-3) = 2$
@answer $2$
:::

:::mistake Going left to right
In $-7 + 4 \times (-2)$, adding first gives $-3 \times (-2) = 6$. That is wrong. Multiply and divide **before** you add and subtract.
:::

:::

## Integers at work and at home

Two common jobs for integers are finding an average temperature and keeping track of money you owe.

:::definition Mean
The **mean** (or average) is the total of the numbers divided by how many numbers there are.
:::

:::figure
{"type":"table","headers":["Night","Low (°C)"],"rows":[["Mon","-12"],["Tue","-8"],["Wed","-15"],["Thu","-5"],["Fri","-10"]]}
:::

:::example Mean overnight low
The table shows the overnight lows in Fort McMurray for five nights. What is the mean low?
@step Add the temperatures: $-12 + (-8) + (-15) + (-5) + (-10) = -50$
@step Divide by the number of nights: $-50 \div 5 = -10$
@answer The mean low is $-10^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::example Paying back a loan
Owen borrowed \$150 from his aunt, so his balance is $-150$. He pays back \$25 a week for $4$ weeks. What is his balance now?
@step Write the calculation: $-150 + 4 \times 25$
@step Multiply first: $4 \times 25 = 100$
@step Then add: $-150 + 100 = -50$
@answer His balance is $-50$. Owen still owes \$50.
:::

:::check m204-integer-ops 2
:::

:::check m204-integer-ops 3
:::

:::summary
- An integer is a positive or negative whole number, or zero.
- Adding a negative is the same as subtracting. Subtracting a negative is the same as adding.
- To multiply or divide: same signs give a positive answer, different signs give a negative answer.
- Order of operations (BEDMAS): brackets, exponents, multiply and divide, then add and subtract.
- The mean is the total divided by how many numbers there are.
:::
