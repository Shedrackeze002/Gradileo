A culture of 100 bacteria that doubles every hour holds more than 100 000 bacteria after just 10 hours, because $100 \times 2^{10} = 102\,400$. When the variable is in the exponent, a function grows (or shrinks) by the same *factor* each step, not by the same amount. These are exponential functions.

## Exponential functions

:::definition Exponential function
A function of the form $y = c^{x}$, where the base $c$ is a constant with $c > 0$ and $c \ne 1$, and the exponent $x$ is the variable.
:::

The base is restricted for good reasons. If $c = 1$, then $1^{x} = 1$ for every $x$: a horizontal line, not growth. If $c < 0$, powers such as $(-4)^{\frac{1}{2}}$ are not real numbers, so the graph would have gaps.

A table of values shows the pattern. For $y = 2^{x}$, each time $x$ increases by $1$, $y$ doubles:

| $x$ | $-2$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y = 2^{x}$ | $\frac{1}{4}$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $1$ | $2$ | $4$ | $8$ |

:::example Evaluate an exponential function
For $f(x) = 4^{x}$, find $f(-3)$ and $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$.
@step A negative exponent means a reciprocal: $f(-3) = 4^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{4^{3}} = \dfrac{1}{64}$.
@step An exponent of $\frac{1}{2}$ means a square root: $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 4^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{4} = 2$.
@answer $f(-3) = \frac{1}{64}$ and $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 2$
:::

- When $c > 1$, the function is **increasing**: exponential **growth**.
- When $0 < c < 1$, the function is **decreasing**: exponential **decay**.

The two graphs are reflections of each other in the $y$-axis, because $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{x} = \left(2^{-1}\right)^{x} = 2^{-x}$. In general, $\left(\frac{1}{c}\right)^{x} = c^{-x}$.

:::example Growth or decay?
Classify $y = 0.75^{x}$, $y = 3^{-x}$ and $y = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{-x}$.
@step $y = 0.75^{x}$: the base $0.75$ is between $0$ and $1$, so it shows decay.
@step $y = 3^{-x} = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{x}$: the base is $\frac{1}{3}$, so it shows decay.
@step $y = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{-x} = 3^{x}$: the base is $3$, so it shows growth.
@answer Decay, decay and growth.
:::

## Key characteristics

Every graph of $y = c^{x}$ shares these features:

- **Domain:** $\{x \mid x \in \mathbb{R}\}$ — any real number can be an exponent.
- **Range:** $\{y \mid y > 0,\ y \in \mathbb{R}\}$ — a positive base to any power is positive.
- **$y$-intercept:** $1$, because $c^{0} = 1$. There is no $x$-intercept.
- **Horizontal asymptote:** $y = 0$ (the $x$-axis).
- It passes through $(1, c)$ and $\left(-1, \frac{1}{c}\right)$.

:::definition Asymptote
A line that a graph approaches more and more closely but never reaches. For $y = c^{x}$, the values of $y$ get closer and closer to $0$ as $x$ moves to one side, but never equal $0$.
:::

:::example Describe the graph
State the characteristics of $y = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{x}$.
@step The base $\frac{1}{3}$ is between $0$ and $1$: the function is decreasing (decay).
@step $y$-intercept: $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{0} = 1$. It also passes through $\left(1, \frac{1}{3}\right)$ and $(-1, 3)$.
@step As $x$ increases, $y$ approaches $0$, so the asymptote is $y = 0$.
@answer Domain $\{x \mid x \in \mathbb{R}\}$, range $\{y \mid y > 0,\ y \in \mathbb{R}\}$, $y$-intercept $1$, asymptote $y = 0$, decreasing.
:::

## Finding an exponential equation

For $y = a(c)^{x}$, the $y$-intercept is $a$ (since $c^{0} = 1$), and every increase of $1$ in $x$ multiplies $y$ by $c$. Two points are enough to find both values: divide one equation by the other so that $a$ cancels.

:::example Equation through two points
An exponential function $y = a(c)^{x}$ passes through $(1, 6)$ and $(3, 54)$. Find its equation.
@step Substitute: $6 = ac$ and $54 = ac^{3}$.
@step Divide: $\dfrac{ac^{3}}{ac} = \dfrac{54}{6}$, so $c^{2} = 9$ and $c = 3$ (the base is positive).
@step Then $a = \dfrac{6}{3} = 2$.
@answer $y = 2(3)^{x}$
:::

:::example Reading a model
The population of a small town is modelled by $P = 8400(1.025)^{t}$, where $t$ is the number of years since 2020. Interpret the model.
@step When $t = 0$, $P = 8400$: the population in 2020.
@step The factor $1.025 = 1 + 0.025$, so each year the population is multiplied by $1.025$: it grows by 2.5% per year.
@answer It starts at 8400 people and grows by 2.5% per year.
:::

:::tip Growth and decay rates
A factor of $1 + r$ means growth at rate $r$; a factor of $1 - r$ means decay at rate $r$. So $0.86$ means a decrease of 14% per period, not 86%.
:::

:::summary
- An exponential function has the form $y = c^{x}$ with $c > 0$, $c \ne 1$.
- $c > 1$ gives growth (increasing); $0 < c < 1$ gives decay (decreasing); $\left(\frac{1}{c}\right)^{x} = c^{-x}$.
- Domain: all real numbers. Range: $y > 0$. $y$-intercept $1$. Horizontal asymptote $y = 0$.
- For $y = a(c)^{x}$, $a$ is the $y$-intercept; divide two equations to find $c$.
:::
