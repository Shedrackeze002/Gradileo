Tips at a restaurant, sales at the mall and the sales tax on a receipt are all percents. If you can find a percent of an amount, you can check your bill, compare deals and plan your money.

## Finding a percent of an amount

To find a percent of an amount, change the percent to a decimal. Then multiply.

:::figure
{"type":"circleGraph","slices":[{"label":"Bus","value":9},{"label":"Walk","value":18},{"label":"Car","value":18}],"show":"percent","caption":"How 45 students get to school"}
:::

:::example 20% of 45 students
There are 45 students in a class. $20\%$ of them take the bus. How many students take the bus?
@step Change the percent to a decimal: $20\% = 0.20$
@step Multiply: $0.20 \times 45 = 9$
@answer 9 students take the bus.
:::

:::tip Mental math shortcuts
- $10\%$: divide by 10. $10\%$ of \$80 is \$8.
- $50\%$: divide by 2. $25\%$: divide by 4.
- $5\%$ is half of $10\%$. $20\%$ is double $10\%$.
:::

:::mistake Multiplying by the whole percent number
$20 \times 45 = 900$ students. That is far more than the whole class! Change $20\%$ to $0.20$ before you multiply.
:::

## Tips

:::definition Tip
A **tip** is extra money you give for good service, for example at a restaurant or a hair salon. Many people tip $15\%$ to $20\%$ of the bill.
:::

:::example A 15% tip
Hamid's dinner bill is \$46.80. He wants to leave a $15\%$ tip. How much is the tip, and what is the total?
@step $15\% = 0.15$
@step Tip: $0.15 \times 46.80 = 7.02$
@step Total: $46.80 + 7.02 = 53.82$
@answer The tip is \$7.02, and the total is \$53.82.
:::

:::tip Check a 15% tip in your head
$15\%$ is $10\%$ plus half of $10\%$. For \$46.80: $10\%$ is \$4.68, half of that is \$2.34, and $4.68 + 2.34 = 7.02$.
:::

## Discounts and sale prices

:::definition Discount and sale price
A **discount** is the amount taken off the price. The **sale price** is what you pay after the discount.
$$\text{sale price} = \text{price} - \text{discount}$$
:::

:::example 30% off a jacket
A winter jacket costs \$89.99. It is on sale for $30\%$ off. What is the sale price?
@step Discount: $0.30 \times 89.99 = 26.997$, which rounds to \$27.00.
@step Sale price: $89.99 - 27.00 = 62.99$
@answer The sale price is \$62.99.
:::

:::tip A one-step shortcut
With $30\%$ off, you pay $70\%$ of the price: $0.70 \times 89.99 = 62.993$, or about \$62.99.
:::

:::mistake Stopping at the discount
The discount (\$27.00) is what you **save**, not what you pay. Subtract it from the price to get the sale price.
:::

## Sales tax (GST)

:::definition Sales tax (GST)
Many places add a **sales tax** to the price of most things you buy, right at the till. Depending on where you live, it may be called sales tax, GST (Goods and Services Tax) or VAT, and the rate is different from place to place. In this course the tax is called the **GST**, and it is $5\%$. Each question tells you the rate.
:::

:::example Adding GST
Kai buys work boots for \$129.99. $5\%$ GST is added. What is the total?
@step GST: $0.05 \times 129.99 = 6.4995$, which rounds to \$6.50.
@step Total: $129.99 + 6.50 = 136.49$
@answer The total is \$136.49.
:::

:::tip GST in one step
Multiplying by $1.05$ adds $5\%$ in one step: $129.99 \times 1.05 = 136.4895$, or about \$136.49.
:::

:::check m104-discount-tax 2
:::

When an item is on sale, the store takes off the discount **first**. Then it adds GST to the sale price.

:::example Sale price, then GST
A cordless drill costs \$149.99 and is $20\%$ off. Then $5\%$ GST is added. What is the total?
@step Discount: $0.20 \times 149.99 = 29.998$, which rounds to \$30.00.
@step Sale price: $149.99 - 30.00 = 119.99$
@step GST: $0.05 \times 119.99 = 5.9995$, which rounds to \$6.00.
@step Total: $119.99 + 6.00 = 125.99$
@answer The total is \$125.99.
:::

The receipt shows each step.

:::mistake GST on the old price
Find the GST on the price you actually pay, which is the sale price. GST on \$149.99 would be too much.

:::summary
- To find a percent of an amount, change the percent to a decimal and multiply.
- Tip $=$ percent $\times$ bill. Total $=$ bill $+$ tip.
- Sale price $=$ price $-$ discount. With $30\%$ off, you pay $70\%$ of the price.
- GST is $5\%$: multiply by $0.05$ and add, or multiply by $1.05$.
- With a sale, take off the discount first, then add GST.
:::
