An analytical essay does not retell a text. It makes a claim about how the text works, and then proves it. In this lesson you will write an arguable **analytical thesis** and **structure** an essay around it.

## The analytical thesis

:::definition Analytical thesis
An **analytical thesis** is an interpretation of a text that the essay sets out to prove. It argues what a text means or how it works, not just what happens in it.
:::

| Weak | Why | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| *The Tempest* is a play by Shakespeare about an island. | a fact, with nothing to prove | Shakespeare uses Caliban to question whether Prospero’s rule is just. |
| Owen’s poem is about war, and war is bad. | vague and obvious | Owen’s imagery of disease and decay turns the soldiers’ heroism into horror. |

A strong thesis is **arguable** (someone could disagree) and **precise**: it names the text, the writer’s method and the effect.

:::example From “what” to “how and why”
@step **What:** Prospero forgives his enemies.
@step **How:** Shakespeare builds up Prospero’s power over them, then has him choose mercy.
@step **Why:** to suggest that mercy is a rarer strength than revenge.
@answer Thesis: “By giving Prospero complete power over his enemies before he forgives them, Shakespeare suggests that mercy is a rarer strength than revenge.”
:::

## Structuring the essay

1. **Introduction**: some context on the text and the question, ending with your thesis.
2. **Body paragraphs**: each begins with a **topic sentence** that makes one point supporting the thesis, then gives evidence and analysis.
3. **Transitions**: words such as *however*, *similarly* and *as a result* show how one idea links to the next.
4. **Conclusion**: draws the argument together and answers the question “so what?”

Order your body paragraphs so the argument **builds**, often from simpler points to deeper ones, and save your most insightful point for last.

:::mistake A conclusion is not a full stop
“In conclusion, that is everything I have to say” adds nothing. A strong conclusion shows the bigger significance: “In forgiving his enemies, Prospero shows that the play’s real magic is mercy.” Don’t introduce brand-new evidence there.
:::

:::summary
- An **analytical thesis** is an arguable, precise interpretation: text, method and effect.
- Each body paragraph opens with a **topic sentence**; **transitions** link the ideas.
- The **conclusion** shows why the argument matters.
:::
