Simran scores $+4$ for each correct answer in a math contest and $-2$ for each wrong one. To find her score, you need several operations with integers, done in the right order.

## The order of operations

:::definition Order of operations
1. Do the operations inside **brackets** first.
2. **Multiply and divide** from left to right.
3. **Add and subtract** from left to right.
:::

These are the same rules you use with whole numbers. Now the numbers can be negative, so watch the signs at every step.

:::example Multiply before adding
Evaluate $(-3) + 4 \times (-2)$.
@step Multiply first: $4 \times (-2) = -8$.
@step Then add: $(-3) + (-8) = -11$.
@answer $(-3) + 4 \times (-2) = -11$
:::

:::mistake Working from left to right
Adding first gives $(-3) + 4 = 1$ and then $1 \times (-2) = -2$. That is wrong. Multiplication comes before addition.
:::

:::

## Brackets and longer expressions

Work one step at a time, and write each new line. Copy every sign carefully.

:::example Brackets first
Evaluate $(-12) \div (3 - 7) + 2 \times (-5)$.
@step Brackets: $3 - 7 = -4$. The expression is now $(-12) \div (-4) + 2 \times (-5)$.
@step Divide and multiply from left to right: $(-12) \div (-4) = 3$ and $2 \times (-5) = -10$.
@step Add: $3 + (-10) = -7$.
@answer $(-12) \div (3 - 7) + 2 \times (-5) = -7$
:::

:::example Division and multiplication from left to right
Evaluate $(-18) \div 3 \times (-2)$.
@step Division comes first here because it is on the left: $(-18) \div 3 = -6$.
@step Then multiply: $(-6) \times (-2) = 12$.
@answer $(-18) \div 3 \times (-2) = 12$
:::

:::tip Multiplication does not always go first
Multiplication and division have the **same** rank. Do whichever comes first from the left. Doing $3 \times (-2)$ first in the last example would give $3$, which is wrong.
:::

A fraction bar groups the top and the bottom, just like brackets.

$$\dfrac{(-4) + (-8)}{-3} = \dfrac{-12}{-3} = 4$$

:::check m8-integer-order-ops 2
:::

## Solving problems with integers

Write an expression for the situation. Then use the order of operations.

:::example Contest score
Simran’s contest results are in the table. What is her score?
@step Score $= 9 \times 4 + 6 \times (-2) + 5 \times 0$.
@step Multiply first: $36 + (-12) + 0$.
@step Add: $36 + (-12) + 0 = 24$.
@answer Simran’s score is $24$.
:::

:::example A changing temperature
At 7 a.m. in Westbury, the temperature was $-14^\circ\text{C}$. It rose $3^\circ\text{C}$ per hour for $4$ hours, then fell $2^\circ\text{C}$ per hour for $3$ hours. What was the final temperature?
@step A rise is positive and a fall is negative: $(-14) + 4 \times 3 + 3 \times (-2)$.
@step Multiply first: $(-14) + 12 + (-6)$.
@step Add from left to right: $(-14) + 12 = -2$, then $(-2) + (-6) = -8$.
@answer The final temperature was $-8^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::check m8-integer-order-ops 3
:::

:::summary
- Brackets first, then multiply and divide from left to right, then add and subtract from left to right.
- Multiplication and division have the same rank, and so do addition and subtraction.
- A fraction bar groups the top and the bottom like brackets.
- For a problem, write an expression with integers (gains positive, losses negative), then evaluate it.
:::
