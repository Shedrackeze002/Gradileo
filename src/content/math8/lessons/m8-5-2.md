## Grouping: dividing by a negative integer

$(-12) \div (-4)$ asks: how many groups of $(-4)$ are in $(-12)$?

:::example Making groups
Find $(-12) \div (-4)$.
@step Start with $12$ negative tiles. Make groups of $4$ negative tiles.
@step There are $3$ groups.
@answer $(-12) \div (-4) = 3$
:::

Division undoes multiplication, so you can also use a **related multiplication**.

:::example Using multiplication
Find $12 \div (-4)$.
@step Think: $(-4) \times ? = 12$.
@step $(-4) \times (-3) = 12$, because the signs are the same.
@answer $12 \div (-4) = -3$
:::

## Sign rules for division

:::definition Sign rules for division
Divide the numbers without their signs. Then:
- **same signs** give a **positive** quotient;
- **different signs** give a **negative** quotient.
These are the same rules as for multiplication.
:::

A fraction bar also means divide: $\dfrac{-56}{8} = (-56) \div 8 = -7$.

:::example A missing factor
Find the missing integer: $(-6) \times \square = 42$.
@step The missing factor is $42 \div (-6)$.
@step $42 \div 6 = 7$, and the signs are different, so the quotient is $-7$.
@step Check: $(-6) \times (-7) = 42$.
@answer $\square = -7$
:::

:::mistake Dividing by zero
You can multiply by $0$, but you cannot divide by $0$. $0 \div (-5) = 0$, but $(-5) \div 0$ has no answer.
:::

## Average change and the mean

To find an **average change**, divide the total change by the number of time periods.

:::example Temperature per hour
In Northfield, the temperature fell from $4^\circ\text{C}$ to $-14^\circ\text{C}$ over $6$ hours. It changed by the same amount each hour. What was the change per hour?
@step Total change $=$ final $-$ start $= -14 - 4 = -18$.
@step Change per hour: $(-18) \div 6 = -3$.
@answer The temperature changed by $-3^\circ\text{C}$ per hour.
:::

:::example Mean low temperature
The daily low temperatures in Riverton for five days were $-8^\circ\text{C}$, $-3^\circ\text{C}$, $2^\circ\text{C}$, $-6^\circ\text{C}$ and $-10^\circ\text{C}$. What is the mean low temperature?
@step Add: $(-8) + (-3) + 2 + (-6) + (-10) = -25$.
@step Divide by the number of days: $(-25) \div 5 = -5$.
@answer The mean low temperature is $-5^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::check m8-integer-divide 3
:::

:::summary
- Sharing: $(-12) \div 3$ shares $12$ negative tiles into $3$ groups, so each group is $-4$.
- Grouping: $(-12) \div (-4)$ counts groups of $(-4)$ in $(-12)$, which is $3$.
- Same signs give a positive quotient. Different signs give a negative quotient.
- Average change $=$ total change $\div$ number of periods. The mean is the sum divided by the number of values.
:::
