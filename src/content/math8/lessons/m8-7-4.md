You now know how to solve one-step, two-step and bracket equations. The last skill is turning a real problem into an equation. Once the equation is written, the problem is mostly solved.

## From words to an equation

Use these four steps for any problem:
1. Choose a letter for the unknown, and say what it means.
2. Write an equation that says the same thing as the words.
3. Solve the equation.
4. Check the answer **in the problem**, not just in your equation.

Some words point to operations. "Each" or "per" usually means multiply. A starting fee or a starting amount is added once. "Shared equally" means divide.

:::example A phone bill
Kiran’s phone plan costs \$20 a month plus \$5 for each GB of data. Last month the bill was \$45. How much data did Kiran use?
@step Let $g$ be the number of GB. The bill is \$5 for each GB plus \$20, so $5g + 20 = 45$.
@step Subtract $20$ from both sides: $5g = 25$
@step Divide both sides by $5$: $g = 5$
@step Check in the problem: $5$ GB cost $5 \times 5 = 25$ dollars, plus the \$20 fee is \$45. It works.
@answer Kiran used $5$ GB of data.
:::

:::mistake Mixing up the rate and the fixed amount
Writing $20g + 5 = 45$ would mean \$20 for each GB plus a \$5 fee. The number that goes with the variable is the amount **per** GB.
:::

## Problems with integers

Equations with negative numbers come from temperatures, elevations and money owed.

:::example A rising temperature
At sunrise in Riverton it was $-11^\circ\text{C}$. The temperature rose $3^\circ\text{C}$ each hour until it reached $7^\circ\text{C}$. How many hours did that take?
@step Let $h$ be the number of hours. Start at $-11$ and add $3$ for each hour: $3h - 11 = 7$.
@step Add $11$ to both sides: $3h = 18$
@step Divide both sides by $3$: $h = 6$
@step Check in the problem: $-11 + 3 \times 6 = -11 + 18 = 7$. It works.
@answer It took $6$ hours.
:::

:::example A submarine
A research submarine is at an elevation of $-30$ m. It changes elevation by the same amount every minute, and after $4$ minutes it is at $-54$ m. What is the change in elevation each minute?
@step Let $r$ be the change each minute, in metres: $4r - 30 = -54$.
@step Add $30$ to both sides: $4r = -54 + 30 = -24$
@step Divide both sides by $4$: $r = -24 \div 4 = -6$
@step Check in the problem: $-30 + 4(-6) = -30 - 24 = -54$. It works.
@answer The elevation changes by $-6$ m each minute: the submarine goes down $6$ m a minute.
:::

## Problems with sharing and brackets

:::example Sharing a pizza
Four friends share the cost of a pizza equally. Each one also pays \$3 for a drink, and each friend pays \$10 in total. How much did the pizza cost?
@step Let $p$ be the cost of the pizza. Each share is $\dfrac{p}{4}$, plus \$3: $\dfrac{p}{4} + 3 = 10$.
@step Subtract $3$ from both sides: $\dfrac{p}{4} = 7$
@step Multiply both sides by $4$: $p = 28$
@step Check in the problem: $28 \div 4 = 7$, and $7 + 3 = 10$. It works.
@answer The pizza cost \$28.
:::

:::example Hockey tickets
Three friends each buy a hockey ticket and pay a \$4 service fee per ticket. They spend \$75 in total. What is the price of one ticket before the fee?
@step Let $t$ be the ticket price. Each friend pays $t + 4$, and there are $3$ friends: $3(t + 4) = 75$.
@step Divide both sides by $3$: $t + 4 = 25$
@step Subtract $4$ from both sides: $t = 21$
@step Check in the problem: $21 + 4 = 25$ dollars each, and $3 \times 25 = 75$. It works.
@answer One ticket costs \$21 before the fee.
:::

:::tip Does the answer make sense?
A negative number of hours or a ticket that costs more than the total is a sign of a mistake. Read the question again and check your equation.
:::

:::summary
- Choose a letter for the unknown and say what it stands for.
- Write an equation that matches the words: a rate multiplies the variable, a fixed amount is added once, and equal shares mean dividing.
- Solve the equation, then check the answer in the problem.
- Give the answer in a sentence, with units.
:::
