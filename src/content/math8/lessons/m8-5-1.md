On a cold night in a northern town, the temperature drops $3^\circ\text{C}$ every hour for $4$ hours. The total change is $4 \times (-3)$. In this lesson, you learn to multiply integers like this.

## Multiplying as repeated addition

$3 \times (-4)$ means $3$ groups of $(-4)$. You can add the groups:

$$3 \times (-4) = (-4) + (-4) + (-4) = -12$$

:::example The cold night
The temperature drops $3^\circ\text{C}$ every hour for $4$ hours. What is the total change in temperature?
@step A drop of $3^\circ\text{C}$ is a change of $-3$. There are $4$ hours, so multiply: $4 \times (-3)$.
@step $4 \times (-3) = (-3) + (-3) + (-3) + (-3) = -12$
@answer The temperature changes by $-12^\circ\text{C}$.

## Zero pairs and a negative first factor

:::definition Zero pair
A **zero pair** is one positive tile and one negative tile. Together they make $0$: $(+1) + (-1) = 0$.
:::

A negative first factor means **remove** groups. You can always start with zero pairs, because adding zero pairs does not change the value.

:::example Two negatives
Use zero pairs to find $(-3) \times (-2)$.
@step $(-3) \times (-2)$ means remove $3$ groups of $(-2)$.
@step Start with $6$ zero pairs (value $0$), as in the picture.
@step Remove $3$ groups of $2$ negative tiles. That takes away all $6$ negative tiles.
@step $6$ positive tiles are left.
@answer $(-3) \times (-2) = 6$
:::

You can also order the factors either way: $(-3) \times 4 = 4 \times (-3) = -12$.

## The sign rules

Look at this pattern. Each time the first factor goes down by $1$, the product goes **up** by $2$.

:::figure
{"type":"table","headers":["Product","Value"],"rows":[["3 × (−2)","−6"],["2 × (−2)","−4"],["1 × (−2)","−2"],["0 × (−2)","0"],["(−1) × (−2)","2"],["(−2) × (−2)","4"],["(−3) × (−2)","6"]]}
:::

:::definition Sign rules for multiplication
Multiply the numbers without their signs. Then:
- **same signs** (both positive or both negative) give a **positive** product;
- **different signs** give a **negative** product.
:::

:::example Using the sign rules
Find $(-7) \times (-8)$ and $(-6) \times 9$.
@step $7 \times 8 = 56$. The signs are the same, so $(-7) \times (-8) = 56$.
@step $6 \times 9 = 54$. The signs are different, so $(-6) \times 9 = -54$.
@answer $(-7) \times (-8) = 56$ and $(-6) \times 9 = -54$.
:::

:::mistake Mixing up the rules for adding and multiplying
$(-3) + (-2) = -5$, but $(-3) \times (-2) = 6$. "Two negatives make a negative" is true for adding, not for multiplying.
:::

:::check m8-integer-multiply 2
:::

## More than two factors

Multiply two factors at a time. Or count the negative factors: an **even** number of negatives gives a positive product, and an **odd** number gives a negative product.

:::example Three factors
Find $(-2) \times 3 \times (-5)$.
@step $(-2) \times 3 = -6$
@step $(-6) \times (-5) = 30$
@step Check: there are $2$ negative factors (even), so the product is positive.
@answer $(-2) \times 3 \times (-5) = 30$
:::

:::tip Brackets side by side
$(-4)(7)$ means $(-4) \times 7$. Two brackets with nothing between them mean multiply.
:::

:::check m8-integer-multiply 3
:::

:::summary
- $3 \times (-4)$ means $3$ groups of $(-4)$: $(-4) + (-4) + (-4) = -12$.
- A negative first factor means remove groups. Start with zero pairs.
- Same signs give a positive product. Different signs give a negative product.
- With several factors, an even number of negatives gives a positive product, and an odd number gives a negative product.
:::
