Some poems make you feel calm; others make you feel uneasy, excited or sad. The feeling a poem or story creates in the reader is its **mood**. Writers build mood carefully, through their images, their sounds and even the length of their sentences.

## What mood is

:::definition Mood
**Mood** is the feeling or atmosphere a text creates for the reader: cheerful, gloomy, peaceful, tense, mysterious, frightening and so on.
:::

Mood is not the same as a character’s feelings. A character can be happy while the reader feels nervous, because the reader can see danger the character cannot.

:::example Describing the mood
> The fog comes\
> on little cat feet.
>
> It sits looking\
> over harbor and city\
> on silent haunches\
> and then moves on.
>
> — Carl Sandburg, “Fog” (1916)

What is the mood of this poem?
@step The images are soft and slow: little cat feet, sitting, silent haunches.
@step Nothing loud or sudden happens.
@answer Quiet and calm.
:::

## How words create mood

| Tool | How it builds mood | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Images** | what the writer makes you see, hear and feel | *a cold draught crept across the floor* |
| **Word choice** | words with strong feelings attached | *gloomy, sparkling, creaked, gently* |
| **Sound** | harsh or soft sounds | *crash, clatter* or *hush, murmur* |
| **Sentence length** | short sentences create tension; long ones can feel calm | *Tap. Tap. Tap. Then silence.* |

:::example Finding the words
> The candle flickered and went out. Somewhere in the dark house a door creaked open, a cold draught crept across the floor, and the footsteps on the stairs stopped.

Which words create a frightening mood?
@step Darkness and a creaking door are unsettling sounds and sights.
@step A cold draught that *crept* feels as if something is sneaking up.
@answer *creaked*, *cold draught* and *crept* (along with *dark* and the footsteps that suddenly stop).
:::

Mood can also **change**. Wordsworth’s speaker begins “lonely as a cloud”, but the sight of the dancing daffodils fills him with delight.

:::summary
- **Mood** is the feeling a text creates in the reader.

- Mood can change during a poem or story.
:::
