Every word in a sentence has a job. Some words name things, some tell what happens, and some describe. These jobs are called the **parts of speech**. Knowing them helps you build clear sentences and fix ones that sound wrong.

## Five parts of speech

| Part of speech | Its job | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Noun** | names a person, place, thing or idea | *girl, Toronto, pencil, kindness* |
| **Pronoun** | takes the place of a noun | *I, you, he, she, it, we, they, him, her, them* |
| **Verb** | shows an action or a state of being | *run, jump, think, is, was* |
| **Adjective** | describes a noun: what kind, which one, how many | *tall, blue, three, noisy* |
| **Adverb** | describes a verb: how, when or where | *quickly, softly, yesterday, outside* |

:::example Labelling a sentence
> The tired dog slept peacefully.

Name the part of speech of each word after *The*.
@step *dog* names an animal: a noun. *tired* describes the dog: an adjective.
@step *slept* is what the dog did: a verb. *peacefully* tells how it slept: an adverb.
@answer tired (adjective), dog (noun), slept (verb), peacefully (adverb).
:::

:::note The same word can do different jobs
A word’s part of speech depends on its job in the sentence. In *We* ***play*** *outside*, *play* is a verb. In *We watched a* ***play***, it is a noun.
:::

## Adjective or adverb?

Adjectives describe **nouns**. Adverbs describe **verbs**. Many adverbs are made by adding *-ly* to an adjective: *quiet* becomes *quietly*.

:::example Choosing the right word
> Sam sang \_\_\_\_\_ at the concert. (*beautiful* or *beautifully*?)

@step The word describes *sang*, which is a verb.
@step A word that describes a verb is an adverb.
@answer beautifully
:::

:::mistake Good and well
*Good* is an adjective; *well* is the adverb. Say *a* ***good*** *game* (describing the noun *game*), but *she played* ***well*** (describing the verb *played*). “She played good” is a common mistake.
:::

:::summary
- **Nouns** name; **pronouns** replace nouns; **verbs** show action or being.
- **Adjectives** describe nouns; **adverbs** describe verbs (how, when, where).
- Many adverbs end in *-ly*; *well* is the adverb for *good*.
:::
