Long before there were books, people told stories aloud around fires and at bedtime. Many of those stories were passed down for hundreds of years. In this lesson you will meet the main kinds of traditional tales, and learn to find the **lesson** a fable teaches.

## Fables and their morals

:::definition Fable and moral
A **fable** is a short story, often with animals that talk and act like people, that teaches a **moral**: a lesson about how to live. The most famous fables are said to have been told by **Aesop**, a storyteller in ancient Greece.
:::

:::example The Fox and the Grapes
> A hungry fox saw a bunch of grapes hanging high on a vine. He jumped and jumped but could not reach them. At last he walked away, saying, “I’m sure they were sour anyway.”
>
> — retold from Aesop

What is the lesson of this fable?
@step What does the fox want, and does he get it? He wants the grapes but cannot reach them.
@step What does he do then? He pretends he never wanted them.
@answer The moral: people often pretend they do not want what they cannot have.
:::

:::mistake A moral is a lesson, not a fact from the story
“Foxes like grapes” is a fact about this story. A moral is a lesson for **people**: it would still be true in your own life.
:::

## Kinds of traditional tales

| Kind | What it is like | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Fable** | Short, often with talking animals; teaches a moral | “The Lion and the Mouse” |
| **Fairy tale** | Magic, royalty, good and evil; often begins “Once upon a time” | “The Elves and the Shoemaker” (Brothers Grimm) |
| **Folk tale** | Passed down by people telling it aloud | “Stone Soup” |
| **Myth** | Explains how something in nature began, often with gods or heroes | Greek myths of the seasons |
| **Tall tale** | A larger-than-life hero, with hugely exaggerated deeds | A farmer who digs a lake with one scoop |

:::summary
- A **fable** is a short tale, often with animals, that teaches a **moral**.
- A moral is a lesson for life, not just a fact from the story.
- **Fairy tales** have magic; **myths** explain nature; **tall tales** exaggerate; **folk tales** were passed down by telling.
