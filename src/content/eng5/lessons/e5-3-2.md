Informational books and websites are built to help you find things quickly. Headings, pictures, bold words and lists at the back of a book are called **text features**. Knowing what each one does saves you from reading every word to find one fact.

## The main text features

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | names the whole text |
| **Heading** | names what one section is about |
| **Bold** or *italic* words | mark important words, often defined nearby or in the glossary |
| **Photograph** and **caption** | the caption, under a picture, explains what it shows |
| **Diagram** and **labels** | a drawing with labelled parts shows how something is built or works |
| **Map** | shows where places are |
| **Timeline** | shows events in the order they happened, with dates |
| **Table of contents** | at the front: the chapters and the pages they start on |
| **Glossary** | at the back: important words and their meanings, in ABC order |
| **Index** | at the back: topics in ABC order with every page they appear on |

## Choosing the right feature

:::example Where would you look?
You are reading a book about the human body. You want to know what the word *cartilage* means.

@step The word is a special science word, so it may be **bold** in the text.
@step Lists of special words and their meanings are found in the glossary.
@answer The glossary.
:::

:::example Index or table of contents?
You want to find every page in a book about animals that mentions *penguins*.

@step The table of contents lists only chapters, such as “Birds”.
@step The index lists small topics, such as *penguins*, with every page number.
@answer The index.
:::

:::mistake Glossary and index are both at the back
The **glossary** tells you what a word *means*. The **index** tells you *where* a topic is. If you want a meaning, use the glossary; if you want a page number, use the index.
:::

:::summary
- **Headings** tell you what a section is about; **captions** explain pictures.
- **Diagrams** show parts; **maps** show places; **timelines** show events in order.
- The **table of contents** lists chapters; the **glossary** gives meanings; the **index** lists pages for each topic.
:::
