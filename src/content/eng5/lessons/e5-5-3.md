Verbs change their form to match **who** is doing something and **when** it happens. *The dog barks*, *the dogs bark*, *the dog barked*: the verb changes each time. In this lesson you will learn to make verbs agree with their subjects, and to use the right tense.

## Subject-verb agreement

:::definition Agreement
A verb must **agree** with its subject. In the present tense:
- a **singular** subject (one) takes a verb ending in **-s**: *The bird* ***sings***.
- a **plural** subject (more than one) takes a verb **without -s**: *The birds* ***sing***.
:::

*I* and *you* take the form without *-s*: *I sing*, *you sing*. The verb *be* has its own forms: *I am*, *he is*, *they are*; *he was*, *they were*.

| Singular | Plural |
|---|---|
| The puppy **runs**. | The puppies **run**. |
| She **is** ready. | They **are** ready. |
| My friend **has** a bike. | My friends **have** bikes. |

:::example Words between the subject and the verb
> The box of crayons (is / are) on the shelf.

@step Find the subject: what is on the shelf? The **box** is.
@step *of crayons* only describes the box. *Box* is singular.
@answer The box of crayons **is** on the shelf.
:::

:::mistake Two subjects joined by "and"
Two subjects joined by *and* make a plural: *Ana and Ben* ***are*** *here*, not *is*.
:::

## Verb tense

The **tense** of a verb tells **when** something happens.

| Tense | When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Past | already happened | *We* ***walked*** *to school.* |
| Present | happening now, or usually | *We* ***walk*** *to school.* |
| Future | will happen | *We* ***will walk*** *to school.* |

Most verbs form the past by adding *-ed*. **Irregular** verbs change in other ways, and they have to be learned.

| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| go | went |
| eat | ate |
| run | ran |
| catch | caught |
| bring | brought |
| write | wrote |

:::example Using an irregular past
> Yesterday Kai (catch) a huge fish.

@step *Yesterday* tells us it is the past.
@step *Catch* is irregular: its past form is not *catched*.
@answer Yesterday Kai **caught** a huge fish.
:::
