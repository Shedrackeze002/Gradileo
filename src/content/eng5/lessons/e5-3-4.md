A careful reader asks two questions about everything they read: *Is this true, or is it just what someone thinks?* and *Why did the author write this?* In this lesson you will learn to tell **facts** from **opinions**, and to name an **author’s purpose**.

## Fact or opinion?

:::definition Fact and opinion
- A **fact** can be **proved** true or false, by measuring, counting, looking it up or seeing it: *Our school has 312 students.*
- An **opinion** tells what someone **thinks, feels or believes**. People can disagree with it: *Our school has the best playground in town.*
:::

Opinion clue words include *best, worst, beautiful, boring, should, I think, I believe* and *everyone loves*.

| Fact | Opinion |
|---|---|
| A spider has eight legs. | Spiders are creepy. |
| The library opens at 9 a.m. | The library should open earlier. |
| Water freezes at 0 °C. | Winter is the best season. |

:::example Testing a sentence
> Soccer is the most exciting sport in the world.

Fact or opinion?
@step Could we prove it with a measurement or a reference book? No: *exciting* depends on the person.
@step Many people would disagree.
@answer An opinion.
:::

:::mistake A wrong fact is still a fact statement
“A spider has six legs” is false, but it is still the kind of statement that can be checked and proved wrong. That makes it a (false) fact statement, not an opinion.
:::

## The author’s purpose

Authors write for three main reasons. An easy way to remember them is **PIE**.

| Purpose | The author wants to | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Persuade** | make you think or do something | an advertisement, a letter asking for a new park |
| **Inform** | teach you facts | a textbook page, a news report, instructions |
| **Entertain** | make you enjoy, laugh or feel | a story, a comic, a funny poem |

:::example Naming the purpose
> Come to Maple Street Bakery! Our cinnamon rolls are the softest in the city, and this week every second roll is free.

What is the author’s purpose?
@step The text uses opinion words (*softest*) and offers a deal.
@step It wants the reader to go to the bakery.
@answer To persuade.
:::

- A **fact** can be proved; an **opinion** is what someone thinks or feels.
