Capital letters and end marks are the signposts of writing. A capital tells the reader that a sentence or a name is starting, and an end mark tells them that the sentence has finished and how to read it. In this lesson you will learn when to use each.

## Capital letters

Use a capital letter for:

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| the first word of a sentence | **T**he game starts at noon. |
| the word *I* | Mom and **I** went shopping. |
| names of people and pets | **A**isha, **M**r. **L**ee, **R**ex |
| names of places | **C**anada, **L**ake **O**ntario, **M**ain **S**treet |
| days, months and holidays | **M**onday, **J**uly, **T**hanksgiving |
| titles of books and films | **C**harlotte’s **W**eb |

:::definition Proper and common nouns
A **proper noun** names one particular person, place or thing, and it takes a capital: *Ottawa*. A **common noun** names any one of a kind, and it does not: *city*.
:::

:::example Finding the missing capitals
> last summer my family drove to banff in alberta.

@step The first word of the sentence: *Last*.
@step *Banff* and *Alberta* are names of places.
@step *summer* is a season, and seasons are not capitalized.
@answer Last summer my family drove to Banff in Alberta.
:::

:::mistake Seasons and general words stay small
*spring*, *summer*, *school* and *river* are common nouns. They get a capital only when they start a sentence or are part of a name: *Riverside School*.
:::

## Four kinds of sentences

| Kind | What it does | End mark | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Statement** | tells something | period (.) | *The library is closed today.* |
| **Question** | asks something | question mark (?) | *Is the library closed today?* |
| **Command** | tells someone to do something | period, or ! if said strongly | *Please close the door.* |
| **Exclamation** | shows strong feeling | exclamation mark (!) | *What a huge wave that was!* |

:::example Naming the kind of sentence
> I wonder where my keys are

What kind of sentence is this?
@step It does not ask the reader anything; it tells what the speaker is wondering.
@answer A statement, so it ends with a period.
:::

:::summary
- Capitalize the first word of a sentence, *I*, and **proper nouns** (names of people, places, days, months and titles).
- Seasons and common nouns are not capitalized.
- **Statements** and most **commands** end with a period, **questions** with a question mark, **exclamations** with an exclamation mark.
:::
