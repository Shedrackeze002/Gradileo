Sodium is a soft metal that fizzes violently in water. Chlorine is a poisonous green gas. Yet when they join, they make sodium chloride, the salt you sprinkle on your food. When atoms of different elements join together, they make a new substance with properties of its own.

## Compounds and molecules

:::definition Compound and molecule
A **compound** is a substance made of two or more elements **chemically joined** together. The atoms are held together by **chemical bonds**.

A **molecule** is a group of two or more atoms joined together.
:::

Water is a compound: each water molecule is two hydrogen atoms joined to one oxygen atom. Carbon dioxide is a compound of carbon and oxygen.

Molecules can be elements too. Oxygen gas is made of molecules of **two oxygen atoms** joined together. Because the atoms are all the same kind, oxygen gas is still an element.

:::example Element or compound?
An oxygen molecule is two oxygen atoms joined together. Is oxygen gas an element or a compound?
@step Count the kinds of atom: there is only one kind, oxygen.
@answer An element. Being made of molecules does not make it a compound.
:::

A compound has its **own properties**, often nothing like those of its elements:

| Elements | Their properties | Compound | Its properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| sodium + chlorine | reactive metal + poisonous green gas | sodium chloride | white solid, safe to eat in small amounts |
| hydrogen + oxygen | gases; hydrogen burns | water | liquid that puts out fires |
| carbon + oxygen | black solid + gas | carbon dioxide | colourless gas |

## Particle diagrams

Chemists draw **particle diagrams** to show what a substance is made of: each atom is a circle, and atoms that are joined touch. You can read a diagram with two questions.

- **Are all the particles the same?** If yes, it is one pure substance. If there are different kinds of particle, it is a **mixture**.
- **How many kinds of atom does each particle have?** One kind: an **element**. Two or more kinds joined: a **compound**.

:::example Reading a diagram
A box holds identical particles, and each one is one carbon atom joined to two oxygen atoms. What does it show?
@step All the particles are the same, so it is one substance.
@step Each particle has two kinds of atom joined together.
@answer A compound (carbon dioxide).
:::

:::example Counting substances
A box of gas holds single argon atoms, nitrogen molecules and oxygen molecules. How many substances are there?
@step There are three different kinds of particle.
@answer Three substances, so the box holds a mixture (like air).
:::

:::summary
- A **compound** has two or more elements chemically joined; a **molecule** is a group of joined atoms.
- Some elements, such as oxygen gas (O₂), are made of molecules.
- A compound's properties are different from those of its elements.
- In a particle diagram: identical particles mean one substance (element or compound); different particles mean a mixture.
:::
