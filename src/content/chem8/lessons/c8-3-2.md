You cannot see atoms rearranging, so how can you tell that a reaction has happened? Chemists look for clues: fizzing, a change of colour, a cloudy solid appearing, or a test tube that suddenly feels warm. In this lesson you will learn these signs, and the names for what goes into and comes out of a reaction.

## Signs of a chemical reaction

:::definition Signs of a reaction
A **chemical reaction** makes new substances. These signs show that one may have happened:

- a **gas** is given off (fizzing or bubbles);
- a **colour change**;
- a solid called a **precipitate** forms when two solutions are mixed;
- a **temperature change**: the mixture gets hotter or colder by itself;
- **light** is given out;
- a new **smell**.
:::

:::example Which sign?
Blue copper sulfate solution is mixed with sodium hydroxide solution, and a pale blue solid appears. Which sign is this?
@step A solid has formed from two solutions.
@answer A precipitate forms.
:::

:::mistake Bubbles are not always a reaction
Water boiling in a pan bubbles, but the bubbles are just steam, water as a gas: a physical change. A sign only counts if a **new** substance could be causing it.
:::

## Reactants and products

:::definition Reactants and products
The **reactants** are the substances you start with. The **products** are the new substances made in the reaction.
:::

When magnesium ribbon burns in air, it gives a dazzling white light and leaves a white powder.

| Reactants | Products |
|---|---|
| magnesium, oxygen (from the air) | magnesium oxide |

When zinc is dropped into hydrochloric acid, it fizzes.

| Reactants | Products |
|---|---|
| zinc, hydrochloric acid | zinc chloride, hydrogen |

:::example Heating limestone
Calcium carbonate (limestone) is heated strongly and breaks down into calcium oxide and carbon dioxide. Name the reactants and products.
@step It starts as calcium carbonate alone.
@answer Reactant: calcium carbonate. Products: calcium oxide and carbon dioxide.
:::

:::summary
- Signs of a reaction: a gas, a colour change, a precipitate, a temperature change, light or a new smell.
- A sign counts only if a new substance has been made; boiling bubbles do not count.
- **Reactants** are the starting substances; **products** are the new substances made.
:::
