Two skills separate a story that is told from a story that is lived: **showing** instead of telling, and controlling the **pace**. Both are about where you spend your words.

## Show, don’t tell

:::definition Show, don’t tell
**Telling** names a feeling or fact: *Kemi was nervous.* **Showing** gives actions and details that let the reader work it out: *Kemi’s hands trembled as she opened the letter.*
:::

Showing pulls readers into the scene, because they experience it instead of being informed about it. Use **actions**, **speech**, **sensory details** and **specific images**.

| Telling | Showing |
|---|---|
| *The room was messy.* | *Socks hung from the lamp, and pizza boxes towered beside the bed.* |
| *Leo was happy.* | *Leo burst through the door, grinning, and spun his little brother round.* |
| *The house had been empty for years.* | *A spider had strung its web across the front door.* |

:::mistake Telling has its place
Showing is best for important moments. For dull stretches of time, telling is better: *Three weeks passed.* Showing every minute of those weeks would bore the reader.
:::

## Pacing

:::definition Pacing
**Pacing** is how fast or slowly a story moves.
:::

| To speed up | To slow down |
|---|---|
| short sentences and quick action | long sentences and detailed description |
| **summary**: covering a lot of time in a sentence (*Over the next month, she practised every day.*) | **scene**: showing a single moment closely, second by second |
| skipping unimportant events | adding thoughts, senses and small actions |

A well-paced story moves quickly through the parts that matter less and slows down for the parts that matter most, especially the climax.

:::example Planning the pace
A story is about a rescue at sea.

@step The trip out to sea is not where the drama is, so summarize it.
@step The storm and the rescue are the heart of the story, so slow down and show them in detail.
@answer Summarize the trip out; slow down the storm and the rescue.
:::

:::summary
- **Telling** names a feeling; **showing** reveals it through actions and details.
- Show the important moments; tell to skip quickly over time.
- **Pacing** is how fast a story moves.
- Short sentences and summary speed it up; detail and close scenes slow it down.
:::
