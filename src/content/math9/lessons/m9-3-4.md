You buy three T-shirts at \$12.75 each, then get a \$15.00 refund. To find your new balance in one step, you need to know which operation comes first. The order of operations works the same way for fractions and decimals as it does for integers.

## The same order as before

:::note Order of operations
1. Brackets
2. Exponents
3. Divide and multiply, from left to right
4. Add and subtract, from left to right
:::

:::example Decimals
Evaluate $-2.5 + 3 \times (-1.2)$.
@step Multiply first: $3 \times (-1.2) = -3.6$
@step Add: $-2.5 + (-3.6) = -6.1$
@answer $-6.1$
:::

:::example Fractions
Evaluate $\frac{1}{2} - \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{3}$.
@step Multiply first: $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$
@step Subtract: $\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2} = 0$
@answer $0$
:::

## Powers and negative numbers

A negative number in brackets that is squared gives a positive result.

:::example A squared fraction
Evaluate $\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} + \frac{3}{4} \div \left(-\frac{3}{2}\right)$.
@step Exponent: $\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} = \frac{1}{4}$
@step Divide by multiplying by the reciprocal: $\frac{3}{4} \times \left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) = -\frac{6}{12} = -\frac{1}{2}$
@step Add: $\frac{1}{4} + \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{2}{4} = -\frac{1}{4}$
@answer $-\frac{1}{4}$
:::

:::example Decimals with a power
Evaluate $(-0.5)^{2} \times 8 - 3.2 \div (-0.4)$.
@step Exponent: $(-0.5)^{2} = 0.25$
@step Multiply and divide from left to right: $0.25 \times 8 = 2$ and $3.2 \div (-0.4) = -8$
@step Subtract: $2 - (-8) = 2 + 8 = 10$
@answer $10$
:::

:::mistake The sign of a square
$\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}$ is **not** $-\frac{1}{4}$. Two negative factors give a positive product: $\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) \times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{4}$.
:::

:::check m9-rational-order-ops 2
:::

## Solving multi-step problems

Many problems become one expression. Write the expression first, then use the order of operations.

:::example A bank balance
Talia's account balance is \$45.20. She buys $3$ T-shirts at \$12.75 each, then gets a refund of \$15.00. What is her new balance?
@step Expression: $45.20 - 3 \times 12.75 + 15.00$
@step Multiply first: $3 \times 12.75 = 38.25$
@step Left to right: $45.20 - 38.25 = 6.95$, then $6.95 + 15.00 = 21.95$
@answer Talia's new balance is \$21.95.
:::

The mean (average) of a set of numbers is the sum divided by how many there are. The fraction bar or division sign comes after the sum, so add first.

:::

:::example Mean temperature
The table shows the low temperatures in a northern town for five days. Find the mean low temperature.
@step Sum: $-8.4 + (-12.1) + (-3.5) + 1.2 + (-6.7) = -29.5$
@step Divide by the number of days: $-29.5 \div 5 = -5.9$
@answer The mean low temperature was $-5.9^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::tip Estimate to check
Most of the temperatures are between $-12$ and $-3$, so a mean of about $-6$ makes sense. An estimate also catches a sign slip on a calculator.
:::

:::

:::summary
- The order of operations is the same for all rational numbers: brackets, exponents, divide and multiply, add and subtract.
- A negative number squared is positive: $(-0.5)^{2} = 0.25$.
- To solve a problem, write one expression, then evaluate it in order.
- The mean is the sum divided by the count: add first, then divide.
:::
