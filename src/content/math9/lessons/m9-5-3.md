A town has two plots in its community garden. If you know an expression for the area of each plot, how do you find the total area, or how much bigger one plot is? You add or subtract polynomials, and the only tool you need is collecting like terms.

## Adding polynomials

To add polynomials, put all the terms together and collect like terms. With algebra tiles, you put both groups of tiles together, remove zero pairs and read what is left.

:::example Add two trinomials
Simplify $(2x^2 + 3x - 4) + (x^2 - 5x + 6)$.
@step Remove the brackets. When you add, every term keeps its sign: $2x^2 + 3x - 4 + x^2 - 5x + 6$
@step Group like terms: $(2x^2 + x^2) + (3x - 5x) + (-4 + 6)$
@step Add the coefficients: $2 + 1 = 3$, $3 - 5 = -2$ and $-4 + 6 = 2$.
@answer $3x^2 - 2x + 2$
:::

You can also line up like terms in columns, the way you add whole numbers.

## Subtracting polynomials

Subtracting a number is the same as adding its opposite: $7 - 3 = 7 + (-3)$. Polynomials work the same way.

:::definition Opposite of a polynomial
The **opposite** of a polynomial has the sign of **every** term changed. The opposite of $2x^2 + 4x - 7$ is $-2x^2 - 4x + 7$.
:::

To subtract a polynomial, add its opposite. With tiles, this is like flipping every tile in the second group from shaded to white or from white to shaded.

:::example Subtract two trinomials
Simplify $(5x^2 - 2x + 3) - (2x^2 + 4x - 7)$.
@step Add the opposite of the second polynomial. Change the sign of every one of its terms: $5x^2 - 2x + 3 - 2x^2 - 4x + 7$
@step Group like terms: $(5x^2 - 2x^2) + (-2x - 4x) + (3 + 7)$
@step Add the coefficients: $5 - 2 = 3$, $-2 - 4 = -6$ and $3 + 7 = 10$.
@answer $3x^2 - 6x + 10$
:::

:::mistake Subtracting only the first term
A common error is to change the sign of the first term only: $5x^2 - 2x + 3 - 2x^2 + 4x - 7 = 3x^2 + 2x - 4$. That is wrong. The minus sign in front of the bracket applies to **every** term inside it, so $+4x$ becomes $-4x$ and $-7$ becomes $+7$.
:::

:::tip Check by substituting
Substitute an easy value such as $x = 1$ into the question and your answer. For the example: $(5 - 2 + 3) - (2 + 4 - 7) = 6 - (-1) = 7$, and $3 - 6 + 10 = 7$. They match, so the answer is very likely right.
:::

## Solving problems

Many measurement problems use addition and subtraction of polynomials. The perimeter is the sum of the sides, so a missing side is the perimeter minus the sides you know.

:::example A missing side
The perimeter of a triangular sign is $(4x^2 + 9x + 5)$ cm. Two of its sides measure $(x^2 + 3x + 1)$ cm and $(2x^2 + x + 2)$ cm. Write an expression for the third side.
@step Third side $= (4x^2 + 9x + 5) - (x^2 + 3x + 1) - (2x^2 + x + 2)$
@step Change the sign of every term being subtracted: $4x^2 + 9x + 5 - x^2 - 3x - 1 - 2x^2 - x - 2$
@step Group like terms: $(4x^2 - x^2 - 2x^2) + (9x - 3x - x) + (5 - 1 - 2)$
@step Check: $(x^2 + 3x + 1) + (2x^2 + x + 2) + (x^2 + 5x + 2) = 4x^2 + 9x + 5$, which is the perimeter.
@answer The third side is $(x^2 + 5x + 2)$ cm.
:::

:::

:::summary
- To add polynomials, remove the brackets (every term keeps its sign) and collect like terms.
- To subtract a polynomial, add its opposite: change the sign of **every** term in the polynomial being subtracted.
- Check an answer by substituting a simple value, such as $x = 1$, into the question and the answer.
- A missing side is the perimeter minus the known sides.
:::
