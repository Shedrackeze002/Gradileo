If the temperature drops $1.5$ degrees every hour, how much does it change in $4.5$ hours? If a recipe needs $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of milk and you make $2\frac{1}{2}$ batches, how much milk do you need? Questions like these need multiplication and division of rational numbers.

## The sign rules

Multiplying and dividing rational numbers uses the same sign rules as integers.

:::note Sign rules for multiplying and dividing
- **Same signs:** the answer is positive. $(-0.6) \times (-0.5) = 0.3$
- **Different signs:** the answer is negative. $-1.5 \times 4 = -6$
:::

So find the sign first, then work with the sizes.

:::example Dividing decimals
Find $-7.2 \div 0.9$.
@step Sign: the signs are different, so the answer is negative.
@step Size: $7.2 \div 0.9 = 72 \div 9 = 8$
@answer $-7.2 \div 0.9 = -8$
:::

:::example Multiplying two negatives
Find $-0.6 \times (-0.5)$.
@step Sign: the signs are the same, so the answer is positive.
@step Size: $6 \times 5 = 30$, and there are two decimal places in all, so $0.6 \times 0.5 = 0.30$.
@answer $-0.6 \times (-0.5) = 0.3$
:::

## Multiplying and dividing fractions

To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators. To divide by a fraction, multiply by its **reciprocal** (flip it over).

:::example Multiplying fractions
Find $-\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4}$.
@step Sign: different signs, so the product is negative.
@step Sizes: $\frac{2 \times 3}{3 \times 4} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$
@answer $-\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = -\frac{1}{2}$
:::

:::example Dividing fractions
Find $-\frac{3}{5} \div \left(-\frac{9}{10}\right)$.
@step Multiply by the reciprocal: $-\frac{3}{5} \times \left(-\frac{10}{9}\right)$
@step Sign: two negatives give a positive.
@step Sizes: $\frac{3 \times 10}{5 \times 9} = \frac{30}{45} = \frac{2}{3}$
@answer $-\frac{3}{5} \div \left(-\frac{9}{10}\right) = \frac{2}{3}$
:::

:::tip Simplify first
Divide out common factors before you multiply. In $\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{10}{9}$, the $3$ and $9$ share a factor of $3$, and the $10$ and $5$ share a factor of $5$: $\frac{1}{1} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$.
:::

:::mistake Flip the second fraction only
To divide, flip the **second** fraction (the divisor), not the first. $\frac{1}{2} \div \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 = 2$, not $\frac{1}{8}$.
:::

## Mixed numbers and a calculator

Write mixed numbers as improper fractions before you multiply or divide.

:::example Mixed numbers
Find $-1\frac{1}{2} \times 2\frac{2}{3}$.
@step Improper fractions: $-\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{8}{3}$
@step Sign: negative. Sizes: $\frac{3 \times 8}{2 \times 3} = \frac{24}{6} = 4$
@answer $-1\frac{1}{2} \times 2\frac{2}{3} = -4$
:::

For decimals that are hard to work with by hand, use a calculator. Decide the sign first so you can check the display.

:::example Rounding a quotient
Use a calculator to find $-3.75 \div 1.6$, to the nearest hundredth.
@step Sign: different signs, so the answer is negative.
@step $3.75 \div 1.6 = 2.34375$
@answer $-3.75 \div 1.6 \approx -2.34$
:::

## Solving problems

:::example A cold front
The temperature in Pinewood drops $1.5$ degrees each hour for $4.5$ hours. What is the total change in temperature?
@step A drop is negative: the change each hour is $-1.5^\circ\text{C}$.
@step Total change: $-1.5 \times 4.5 = -6.75$
@answer The temperature changes by $-6.75^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::example Scaling a recipe
A recipe for pancakes uses $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of milk. Arjun makes $2\frac{1}{2}$ times the recipe. How much milk does he need?
@step $\frac{3}{4} \times 2\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{5}{2}$
@step $= \frac{15}{8} = 1\frac{7}{8}$
@answer Arjun needs $1\frac{7}{8}$ cups of milk.
:::

:::check m9-rational-multiply-divide 3
:::

:::summary
- Same signs give a positive product or quotient. Different signs give a negative one.
- Multiply fractions straight across. To divide, multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor.
- Write mixed numbers as improper fractions first, and simplify before multiplying.
- Find the sign before you use a calculator, then check the display.
:::
