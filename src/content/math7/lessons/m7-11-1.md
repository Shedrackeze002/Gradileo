Will the spinner land on red? Will your name be drawn for the prize? **Probability** is a number that tells how likely something is. In this lesson you will write probabilities in three ways: as a ratio, a fraction and a percent.

## Outcomes, events and probability

:::definition Outcome, event and favourable outcome
An **outcome** is one possible result of an experiment, like rolling a $4$ on a die. An **event** is one outcome or a group of outcomes, like rolling an even number. A **favourable outcome** is an outcome that fits the event.
:::

When all the outcomes are equally likely, you can find the probability of an event by counting.

:::definition Probability
$$P(\text{event}) = \dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of possible outcomes}}$$
A probability is always from $0$ (impossible) to $1$ (certain).
:::

:::example Picking a marble
A bag has $3$ red, $5$ blue and $2$ green marbles. Jun picks one marble without looking. What is the probability that it is blue?
@step Favourable outcomes: $5$ blue marbles.
@step Total outcomes: $3 + 5 + 2 = 10$ marbles.
@step $P(\text{blue}) = \dfrac{5}{10}$
@answer $P(\text{blue}) = \dfrac{5}{10} = \dfrac{1}{2}$
:::

## Three ways to write a probability

:::figure
{"type":"circleGraph","slices":[{"label":"Blue","value":3},{"label":"Orange","value":5}],"show":"none"}
:::

Think of this spinner as $8$ equal sections: $3$ of them are blue and $5$ are orange. The probability of landing on blue can be written three ways.

- **Ratio:** favourable : total, so $3 : 8$
- **Fraction:** favourable over total, so $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- **Percent:** divide, then multiply by $100$: $3 \div 8 = 0.375$, so $37.5\%$

All three say the same thing: about $3$ spins out of every $8$ should land on blue.

:::example Writing a probability three ways
Each letter of the word MAGICAL is written on a tile. Aiyana picks one tile without looking. Write the probability of picking an A as a ratio, a fraction and a percent.
@step There are $7$ tiles, and $2$ of them show A.
@step Ratio: $2 : 7$. Fraction: $\dfrac{2}{7}$.
@step Percent: $2 \div 7 \approx 0.2857$, and $0.2857 \times 100 \approx 28.57\%$.
@answer $2 : 7$, $\dfrac{2}{7}$, or about $29\%$ (to the nearest whole percent)
:::

:::mistake Favourable : not favourable
The ratio for a probability compares the favourable outcomes with **all** the outcomes. For the spinner, $3 : 5$ compares blue with orange. That is not the probability of blue. The probability is $3 : 8$.
:::

## Changing from one form to another

A ratio $a : b$ is the same as the fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$. A percent is a number out of $100$. Always write a fraction in **lowest terms**: divide the top and bottom by their greatest common factor.

:::example From a percent
The probability that a sunflower seed sprouts is $85\%$. Write it as a fraction in lowest terms and as a ratio.
@step $85\% = \dfrac{85}{100}$
@step The greatest common factor of $85$ and $100$ is $5$: $\dfrac{85 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \dfrac{17}{20}$
@answer $\dfrac{17}{20}$, or $17 : 20$
:::

:::example From a ratio
The probability that a randomly chosen student walks to school is $6 : 15$. Write it as a fraction in lowest terms and as a percent.
@step As a fraction: $\dfrac{6}{15} = \dfrac{6 \div 3}{15 \div 3} = \dfrac{2}{5}$
@step As a percent: $2 \div 5 = 0.4$, and $0.4 \times 100 = 40\%$
@answer $\dfrac{2}{5}$, or $40\%$
:::

:::tip Rounding a percent
Some fractions give a decimal that never ends, like $\dfrac{1}{3} = 0.333\ldots$ Use a calculator to divide, then round the percent: $\dfrac{1}{3} \approx 33\%$.
:::

## Comparing probabilities

It is hard to compare $2 : 5$ with $\dfrac{9}{20}$. Change every probability to the same form first. Percents are usually the easiest to compare.

:::example Which game should you play?
At the fun fair, the probability of winning Ring toss is $2 : 5$, Duck pond is $\dfrac{9}{20}$ and Plinko is $42\%$. Order the games from least likely to most likely to win.
@step Ring toss: $2 : 5$ means $\dfrac{2}{5} = 0.4 = 40\%$
@step Duck pond: $\dfrac{9}{20} = 0.45 = 45\%$
@step Plinko: $42\%$
@answer Ring toss ($40\%$), Plinko ($42\%$), Duck pond ($45\%$). Duck pond gives the best chance.
:::

:::summary
- $P(\text{event}) = \dfrac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}$, from $0$ (impossible) to $1$ (certain).
- Write a probability as a ratio (favourable : total), a fraction in lowest terms, or a percent.
- To get a percent, divide the top by the bottom, then multiply by $100$.
- A ratio for a probability compares favourable outcomes with **all** outcomes, not with the unfavourable ones.
- To compare probabilities, change them all to percents.
:::
