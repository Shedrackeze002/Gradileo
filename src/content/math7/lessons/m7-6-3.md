The temperature in a northern town rose $9^\circ\text{C}$ during the morning and reached $-4^\circ\text{C}$ at noon. What was it at sunrise? An equation, and one balanced step, gives the answer.

## Solutions and inverse operations

:::definition Solution
The **solution** of an equation is the value of the variable that makes the equation true. For $x + 3 = 10$, the solution is $x = 7$, because $7 + 3 = 10$.
:::

:::definition Inverse operation
An **inverse operation** undoes another operation. Adding and subtracting are inverses. Multiplying and dividing are inverses.
:::

To solve an equation, get the variable by itself. Do the inverse operation, and do it to **both sides** so the equation stays balanced.

## Adding and subtracting integers

The same steps work when the numbers are negative. Use what you know about adding and subtracting integers.

:::example Undo adding
Solve $x + 7 = 3$.
@step $7$ is added to $x$. Subtract $7$ from both sides: $x = 3 - 7$
@step $x = -4$
@step Check: $(-4) + 7 = 3$ ✓
@answer $x = -4$
:::

:::example Undo adding a negative
Solve $x + (-5) = 2$.
@step $(-5)$ is added to $x$. Subtract $(-5)$ from both sides: $x = 2 - (-5)$
@step Subtracting $(-5)$ is the same as adding $5$: $x = 2 + 5 = 7$
@step Check: $7 + (-5) = 2$ ✓
@answer $x = 7$
:::

:::example Undo subtracting
Solve $x - 6 = -9$.
@step $6$ is subtracted from $x$. Add $6$ to both sides: $x = (-9) + 6$
@step $x = -3$
@step Check: $(-3) - 6 = -9$ ✓
@answer $x = -3$
:::

:::mistake Doing the same operation instead of the opposite
For $x + 7 = 3$, adding 7 gives $x = 10$. Check it: $10 + 7 = 17$, not $3$. Always use the **inverse** operation, and always check.
:::

## Multiplying and dividing

:::example Undo multiplying
Solve $6x = 42$.
@step $6x$ means $6 \times x$. Divide both sides by $6$: $x = 42 \div 6$
@step $x = 7$
@step Check: $6 \times 7 = 42$ ✓
@answer $x = 7$
:::

:::example Undo dividing
Solve $\dfrac{x}{4} = 9$.
@step $x$ is divided by $4$. Multiply both sides by $4$: $x = 9 \times 4$
@step $x = 36$
@step Check: $36 \div 4 = 9$ ✓
@answer $x = 36$
:::

:::check m7-one-step-equation 2
:::

## Equations in stories

Choose a letter for the unknown. Write an equation that says two amounts are equal, then solve it.

:::example The temperature at sunrise
The temperature rose $9^\circ\text{C}$ and reached $-4^\circ\text{C}$ at noon. What was the temperature at sunrise?
@step Let $t$ be the temperature at sunrise. Then $t + 9 = -4$.
@step Subtract $9$ from both sides: $t = (-4) - 9 = -13$
@step Check: $(-13) + 9 = -4$ ✓
@answer The temperature at sunrise was $-13^\circ\text{C}$.
:::

:::example Hockey tape
Hockey tape comes in packs of 4 rolls. A team needs 36 rolls. How many packs should they buy?
@step Let $p$ be the number of packs. Then $4p = 36$.
@step Divide both sides by $4$: $p = 9$
@step Check: $4 \times 9 = 36$ ✓
@answer The team should buy 9 packs.
:::

:::check m7-one-step-equation 3
:::

:::summary
- The **solution** makes the equation true.
- Undo the operation with its **inverse**: subtract to undo adding, add to undo subtracting, divide to undo multiplying, multiply to undo dividing.
- Do the same thing to both sides, so the equation stays balanced.
- Negative numbers follow the integer rules: $2 - (-5) = 2 + 5$.
- Always check by substituting your answer.
:::
